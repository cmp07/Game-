## 0. Thesis

`03` locks **what** a Fragment is (kind, ports, capacity, tell). This doc locks **how it feels in the hand** — the recover settle, the shelf silhouette, the beat accent — so Fragments never read as purple chronoshards or rarity loot.

**One-line brief:** _Weight in the palm, outline in greyscale, brass tick for tempo — never violet time._

**Still test:** mute audio, desaturate the frame. A stranger names the family from outline alone, and never says “time crystal” or “purple drop.”

**Authority note:** Systems fields in `03` win on ports/capacity/kinds. This doc wins on recover motion, silhouette grammar, and **Pulse / beat color**. Visual materials still defer to [`09_VISUAL.md`](09_VISUAL.md).

## 3. Recover juice — collection without loot

Recover is **craft gather**, not power-up collection. The dopamine is *weight and place*, not *rarity*.

### 3.1 Phrase (four beats)

| Stage | Must show / feel | Must not |
|---|---|---|
| **Notice** | Fragment sits in the field as a readable object; slight shed-light catch on edge | Radar ping, loot beam from sky |
| **Reach** | Hand / cursor tugs with resistance proportional to size tier (S soft, L stubborn) | Instant vacuum on proximity |
| **Settle** | Mass drops into palm / bin; dust or chalk mote; one material tick ([`10_AUDIO.md`](10_AUDIO.md)) | Confetti, XP pop, rarity sting |
| **Home** | Fragment occupies a shelf slot or spindle nest — silhouette stays readable at HUD scale | Stack counter alone; icon strip without outline |

**Carry cap reminder (MVP):** 3–5 loose Fragments. Overflow is a **full hands** comedy beat (refuse + soft knock), never a sell/trash economy prompt.

### 3.2 Juice budget (intentional motions)

Ship these as the Fragment half of the first-session motion set ([`16_FIRST_THIRTY.md`](16_FIRST_THIRTY.md)):

1. **Settle with weight** — brief squash into palm/bin, then rest.  
2. **Shelf nest** — outline snaps to a stamped nest; no bounce-spam.  
3. **Wrong-tug fail** — Fragment stays seated in field; dust puff; teach mass without tip spam.

Supporting (not required every pickup): fiber brush, kiln-warm body for Charge, dry pendulum tick only when a **Pulse** is recovered.

### 3.3 What “collection” may mean

| Allowed | Forbidden |
|---|---|
| Literacy shelf — families you’ve *handled* | Completionist rarity album |
| Size tiers S/M/L named plainly | Stars, skulls, gem borders |
| One scrap residue after clear (`03` §5) | Pity timer / pity purple |
| Gallery of **Structures** you authored | Fragment trophy case as flex inventory |

Collection pride belongs to **Structures you seated** ([`05_STRUCTURES.md`](05_STRUCTURES.md) · [`08_LEGACY.md`](08_LEGACY.md)), not to Fragment hoards.

## 4. Silhouette readability — greyscale grammar

A player should name a kind from **outline alone** after the teach set (`03` §2). Color is secondary; ports are glyphs on the body.

### 4.1 Family silhouette contract (MVP six)

| Family | Outer shape (greyscale) | Capacity read | Port glyph zone | Anti-confusion |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Span** | Long rectangle / cloth strip; length ≫ height | Length + thickness | Ends (`brace`/`brace`) | Not a Channel (no hollow bore) |
| **Anchor** | Peg / stake / wedge mass; wide base or pointed tip | Mass / footprint | Base (`ground`) + top (`brace`) | Not a Charge (no coil/bladder belly) |
| **Channel** | Open trough / culvert U or tube with visible bore | Bore width | Ends (`in`/`out`) | Hollow reads empty — never solid beam |
| **Charge** | Coil, bladder, or kiln-heart bulge | Belly volume | `in` / `out` / `release` cluster | Bulge ≠ Filter mesh |
| **Filter** | Flat baffle / sieve plane with perforations | Mesh density | Face (`in`/`out`) + `sense` notch | Perforations required in silhouette |
| **Pulse** | Pendulum bob + short arm, or bellows folds, or knocker head | Arm length / fold count | `beat` crown + `out` | **No hourglass**; bob ≠ Charge belly |

### 4.2 Readability rules

| Rule | Spec |
|---|---|
| **Greyscale first** | Family distinguishable at 64×64 shelf nest and at field scale with saturation = 0 |
| **One axis of difference** | Prefer shape language over stripe patterns or tiny icons |
| **Port glyphs** | High-contrast stamps on the body; never paragraph tooltips as the primary teach |
| **Size tiers** | S/M/L scale the *same* silhouette — do not invent new outlines per tier |
| **Occlusion** | Overlapping Fragments in a bin keep top outline readable; no rarity glow to separate stacks |
| **Reduce-motion** | Hard cut to nest is OK; never replace silhouette with a color-only flash |

### 4.3 Confusion matrix (must stay green)

| Pair | Failure mode | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Span vs Channel | Both “long” | Channel **must** show hollow bore in outline |
| Anchor vs Charge | Both “chunky” | Anchor = grounded taper; Charge = belly / coil |
| Filter vs Span | Flat strip vs plank | Filter = perforations + face ports |
| Pulse vs Charge | Round bob vs bladder | Pulse keeps **arm / folds / knocker** asymmetry |
| Pulse vs “time loot” | Clock metaphor creep | Ban hourglass; bob is craft weight, not chronometry |

## 5. Beat / tempo color — Time is **not** purple

Pulse Fragments impose **oscillation / beat** on linked parts (`03` §3). That is craft tempo — not chronomancy. The accent color for beat UI, Pulse tells, and Echo-linked tick marks is locked here so art never defaults to violet.

### 5.1 Locked palette (Pulse / beat only)

| Token | Role | Spec (workshop, not neon) |
|---|---|---|
| **`--pulse-brass`** | Primary beat accent | Warm brass / candle-metal — approx `#B08D57` |
| **`--pulse-patina`** | Secondary / resting tick | Oxidized copper-green — approx `#6E8B6E` |
| **`--pulse-ink`** | Pendulum bob / knocker mass | Near-ink brown-black — approx `#2A2420` |
| **`--pulse-flash`** | Single-frame beat confirm | Ochre / kiln ember flash — approx `#C45C26` · ≤2 frames · **never** violet |

**Hard ban in Pulse / beat surfaces:** `#7B5CFF`–family purples, magenta cyber, neon violet bloom, “chrono” blue-purple gradients.

### 5.2 Where the palette may appear

| Allowed | Forbidden |
|---|---|
| Pulse Fragment edge tick / bob highlight | Whole-HUD purple theme |
| Echo Thread beat marks when linked to Pulse | Rarity border for “time-tier” loot |
| Gate / Loom-mark timing stripes (thin brass) | Screen-wide chronomancy wash |
| Audio-visual metronome during inhabit | Rewind afterimage trails |

**Ratio:** beat accent ≤5% of any frame; kiln rust/copper from [`09_VISUAL.md`](09_VISUAL.md) remains the general craft accent. Pulse brass is a **sibling**, not a second identity.

### 5.3 Naming & speech

| Say | Do not say |
|---|---|
| “Pulse,” “beat,” “pendulum,” “tempo,” “knocker” | “Time Fragment,” “chronoshard,” “cooldown crystal” |
| “I needed a slower beat” | “I needed a rare purple” |
| “Brass tick” (art notes) | “Arcane purple” |

**Cliché test (extends `03` §7):** mockups, trailers, and store stills containing Pulse must pass with **zero** purple pixels in the beat accent — brass/patina/ochre only.

## 6. Hand → shelf → seat continuum

Fragments change stance (`loose` → `seated`) without changing identity.

| Stance | Feel | Silhouette duty |
|---|---|---|
| **Field-loose** | Object in place; can be wrong-tugged | Full family outline at world scale |
| **Carried** | Weight in palm / bin; carry cap comedy | Nest silhouette at HUD; ports dim until aim |
| **Provisional seat** | Graph assembling; chalk / slack nearby | Outline stable; no rarity pulse |
| **Tension-seated** | Pressed into cloth/page with Structure ([`05_STRUCTURES.md`](05_STRUCTURES.md) §8) | Family still readable inside the graph |

Seat juice is owned by Structures; Fragment contribution is the **press** — no capture beam, no purple swallow.

## 8. Feel acceptance (~15 min)

1. **Greyscale shelf:** all six MVP families named from nest silhouettes alone.  
2. **Recover phrase:** Notice → Reach → Settle → Home readable without UI text.  
3. **Wrong-tug:** one failed gather teaches mass; no tip spam.  
4. **Pulse color:** beat accents use brass/patina/ochre only — **no purple**.  
5. **Cliché:** zero hourglass / chronoshard / “Fragments of Time” framing in stills.  
6. **Speech:** playtester says “bigger Span” / “slower beat,” not “rare.”  
7. **Structure pride:** collection desire points at seated Structures, not Fragment albums.  
8. **Reduce-motion:** nest + seat remain legible with motion minimized.
