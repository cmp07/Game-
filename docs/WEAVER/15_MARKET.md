## 0. Verdict (read this first)

| Lock | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Shelf** | Premium **puzzle** — minimalist / singleplayer / replay — not horror, idle, coin, or AI-dungeon |
| **Buyer** | Players who want a readable new verb and authorship pride in short sessions |
| **Discovery** | Demo + GIF rewrite slam + wishlist — not influencer lore essays or live-service cadence |
| **Honesty** | Do not claim Echo Lattice wishlists, RACCOIN-scale outliers, or “AI game” trend heat |

**One line:** Sell a fair loom people can show in fifteen seconds — not a genre mash or a hype cycle.

## 1. Positioning

### 1.1 Store sentence (working)

**The Weaver** — a labyrinth that weaves your walk into cloth. Escape by changing your stroke, not by beating chance.

(Working title may ship as Weaver or a later name from `19_NAMES.md`; fantasy stays.)

### 1.2 Tag cluster (MVP)

| Include | Exclude |
|---|---|
| Puzzle, Singleplayer, Minimalist, Indie, Replay Value, Controller | Horror, Survival, Online Co-Op, Free to Play, Sexual Content, Loot, Idle, Card Game |

Exact Steam tag caps are a Partner exercise; this is the **identity filter**.

### 1.3 Category purity

Hard rule from [`../GAME_PLAN.md`](../GAME_PLAN.md) and Concept §2: **one pure category.** Weaver does not absorb coin-pusher, particle idle, or Buckshot-format horror to “widen the funnel.”

## 3. Audience & session

| Trait | MVP target |
|---|---|
| Session length | 15–40 minutes natural stop; daily in ≤20 |
| Skill fantasy | “I authored that pattern,” not “I optimized DPS” |
| Clip behavior | Rewrite slam / portrait ceremony GIF-able mute-on |
| Platform | Windows Steam first; Deck nice-to-have |
| Language | EN first; zh-Hans only if staffing allows — don’t block ship on full l10n |

**Anti-audience (do not chase):** friendslop co-op seekers, gacha collectors, horror adrenaline clips, idle prestige min-maxers.

## 6. Success metrics (small-team honest)

| Metric | Healthy MVP read | Vanity |
|---|---|---|
| Wishlist velocity | Demo/clip driven, steady | Bought spikes |
| Demo→wishlist / purchase | Measurable after Next Fest or evergreen demo | Raw wishlists with 0 demo |
| Review score | “Fair / clever / beautiful page” themes | Review-bomb bait features |
| Refund rate | Low if onboarding teaches the verb | Hidden by day-one −50% only |
| 90-day residual | Dailies + word of mouth | Assumes battle pass |

Do not write a seven-figure ARR forecast into this doc. Ship a credible $5–$9 puzzle; let reviews decide the catalog sequel budget.
