## 0. Thesis

The GDD already locks the verb grammar (Fragment → Thread → Structure), the shed material bible, and the solo offline fence. What is still soft is **how those locks feel in the hand**, how a stranger **discovers** the next stitch without a wiki, and how the product **refuses** purple-void / social-pressure defaults in every still.

**Elevation rule:** each item below is a shippable design bet with a pass/fail test — not a mood board, not a lore essay.

**Five lenses (every elevation tags ≥1):**

| Lens | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Feel** | Weight, ear, motion, authorship pride in the body |
| **Discovery** | How the next idea arrives — in-game or on the Steam shelf |
| **Anti-wiki** | The toy teaches itself; guides feel redundant, not required |
| **Anti-purple** | Fiber / dust / timber / wire / chalk / kiln — never cosmic neon |
| **Solo-first** | Complete loop alone; social residue optional and quiet |

## 1. Twenty-five elevations

### E01 · Collapse comedy as primary teacher
**Lenses:** Feel · Anti-wiki  
**Bet:** Every Tension failure names the culprit joint with one glyph flash + one audible tear, then refunds Fragments with a soft rustle — no modal essay.  
**Ship test:** Cold player recovers from three distinct collapses without opening a help screen.  
**Builds on:** [`02_CORE_LOOP.md`](02_CORE_LOOP.md) §2 · [`04_THREADS.md`](04_THREADS.md) §1 · [`10_AUDIO.md`](10_AUDIO.md)

### E02 · Slack before taut (feel sentence)
**Lenses:** Feel · Anti-wiki  
**Bet:** Provisional Threads always start slack; the climb to taut is a continuous motion+ear phrase the player can interrupt. Teaching fields force one intentional slack joke before the first useful seat.  
**Ship test:** Mute-on trailer still sells “line goes tight” in ≤3 seconds; players describe strain before they say “HP.”  
**Builds on:** [`09_VISUAL.md`](09_VISUAL.md) §3.2 · [`16_FIRST_THIRTY.md`](16_FIRST_THIRTY.md) §2

### E03 · Palm weight on Fragment recover
**Lenses:** Feel · Anti-purple  
**Bet:** Each Fragment family has a settle mass (reed light / timber thud / copper scrape). Recover is a pick-with-weight, never a magnet-loot suck with rarity sparkles.  
**Ship test:** Blind A/B: players sort three families by sound+settle alone ≥80%. Zero purple/chroma pickup VFX.  
**Builds on:** [`03_FRAGMENTS.md`](03_FRAGMENTS.md) · [`16_FIRST_THIRTY.md`](16_FIRST_THIRTY.md) motion budget

### E04 · Gap as antagonist, not skybox
**Lenses:** Anti-purple · Discovery  
**Bet:** Every field’s “void” is a physical scarcity (torn span, starved basin, missing planks). Camera hero-shots the gap; clear color stays warm bone / shed grey — never near-black nebula.  
**Ship test:** Cropped still without UI: stranger says “broken bridge/yard” not “magic ruins.”  
**Builds on:** [`09_VISUAL.md`](09_VISUAL.md) §1 · MASTER glossary “Void (allowed)”

### E05 · One coach line per beat, then silence
**Lenses:** Anti-wiki · Solo-first  
**Bet:** Pedagogy budget = ≤1 diegetic caption per loop beat; no tip carousel, no NPC guide, no quest log novel. Silence after the line is intentional authorship space.  
**Ship test:** First Structure job clearable ≤24:00 with captions alone ([`16_FIRST_THIRTY.md`](16_FIRST_THIRTY.md)); disabling captions still passes for players who finished the prior teach job.  
**Builds on:** [`11_PROGRESSION.md`](11_PROGRESSION.md) literacy layer · [`01_CONCEPT.md`](01_CONCEPT.md) temperature

### E06 · Port glyphs over paragraphs
**Lenses:** Anti-wiki · Feel  
**Bet:** Port legality is a body glyph language (brace notch, feed mouth, oppose vent, echo tick). Tooltips exist as optional long-press, never as the first teach.  
**Ship test:** Player completes Brace→Feed literacy jobs without reading a paragraph tooltip. Illegal snap glyph matches the failing port pair.  
**Builds on:** [`04_THREADS.md`](04_THREADS.md) · [`09_VISUAL.md`](09_VISUAL.md) §3.1

### E07 · Inhabit is the win condition
**Lenses:** Feel · Discovery · Solo-first  
**Bet:** Standing a Structure is ceremony; **using** it clears the job. Build-alone never auto-wins. The first pride beat is walking/routing through your own seam.  
**Ship test:** Vertical-slice jobs reject “decorate then skip”; inhabit path is mandatory and GIF-legible mute-on.  
**Builds on:** [`05_STRUCTURES.md`](05_STRUCTURES.md) · [`02_CORE_LOOP.md`](02_CORE_LOOP.md) beats 4–5

### E08 · Fifteen-second shelf rewrite
**Lenses:** Discovery · Anti-purple  
**Bet:** Store / trailer / short-form open on hands → Thread across a physical gap → Tension seat → inhabit. No lore cold open, no purple key art, no “AI-powered” lead.  
**Ship test:** Mute 0–15s cut reads “parts became place”; capsule passes brand test with title removed ([`09_VISUAL.md`](09_VISUAL.md) shell craft).  
**Builds on:** [`15_MARKET.md`](15_MARKET.md) · MASTER exit criteria #6  
**Aligns market sentence to Yard craft** (closes MASTER §11 market gap temperature).

### E09 · Demo as complete loom vignette
**Lenses:** Discovery · Solo-first  
**Bet:** Demo ships one Shed Yard corner + teach spine to first Structure + one optional mastery job + wishlist CTA. Offline, no account wall.  
**Ship test:** Demo→wishlist path needs zero Discord/wiki; cold player hits CTA after authorship pride, not after a cliffhanger lore dump.  
**Builds on:** [`15_MARKET.md`](15_MARKET.md) §4 · [`17_MVP.md`](17_MVP.md)

### E10 · Kiln copper as the only heat accent
**Lenses:** Anti-purple · Feel  
**Bet:** Stress, Charge, and celebrate moments steal warmth from kiln rust/copper ≤10% of frame — never violet bloom, magenta cyber, or rarity rainbow borders.  
**Ship test:** Palette audit script / art review flags any purple channel dominance in hero frames; overload creep reads rust, not neon.  
**Builds on:** [`09_VISUAL.md`](09_VISUAL.md) §2 · [`03_FRAGMENTS.md`](03_FRAGMENTS.md) §1

### E11 · Workshop hush bed
**Lenses:** Feel · Anti-purple · Solo-first  
**Bet:** Title and Survey beds are shed air / distant kiln / authored silence. Music, if any, enters after first seat — never a mystic void drone or choir destiny bed.  
**Ship test:** First 90s of play contain no orchestra/choir/aether pad; seat phrase is audible over ambient.  
**Builds on:** [`10_AUDIO.md`](10_AUDIO.md) §1

### E12 · Undo Tension as craft courage
**Lenses:** Feel · Anti-wiki · Solo-first  
**Bet:** One-commit undo is framed as re-pinning fiber, not “reload checkpoint.” Players experiment because reverse is cheap and diegetic.  
**Ship test:** ≥50% of teach-field players undo at least once before first clear; abandon never shames ([`08_LEGACY.md`](08_LEGACY.md)).  
**Builds on:** [`02_CORE_LOOP.md`](02_CORE_LOOP.md) fair toy · [`17_MVP.md`](17_MVP.md)

### E13 · Jobs before catalog (Besiege fence)
**Lenses:** Discovery · Anti-wiki  
**Bet:** New Fragment / Structure nouns unlock only when a Yard job *needs* them. No encyclopedia browse that outruns contracts.  
**Ship test:** Vertical slice: jobs ≥ Structures; player never faces a full prefab house menu before first inhabit win.  
**Builds on:** [`11_PROGRESSION.md`](11_PROGRESSION.md) · [`18_RISKS.md`](18_RISKS.md) D3 / Besiege gravity

### E14 · Residue bias as next-field whisper
**Lenses:** Discovery · Solo-first · Anti-wiki  
**Bet:** Clear emits one bias tag that reshapes the next field’s scarcity (scaffold-heavy → longer gap, etc.). The world answers your hand — no loot drop, no purple scrap.  
**Ship test:** Players can name (in their head) how the next field “noticed” them without reading a wiki table.  
**Builds on:** [`08_LEGACY.md`](08_LEGACY.md) §4 · [`07_ECONOMY.md`](07_ECONOMY.md)

### E15 · Local gallery as quiet museum
**Lenses:** Solo-first · Feel · Discovery  
**Bet:** Yard wall shows silhouette stamps of stood Structures — recognition without leaderboards, seasons, or trade flex. Optional ghost self replay is chalk, not a rival invade.  
**Ship test:** Gallery usable fully offline; share is screenshot/seed paste only at MVP.  
**Builds on:** [`08_LEGACY.md`](08_LEGACY.md) · [`12_MULTIPLAYER.md`](12_MULTIPLAYER.md) social residue fence

### E16 · Seed paste, not lobby
**Lenses:** Solo-first · Discovery  
**Bet:** “Multiplayer” surface = daily shared seed + clipboard seed string + friend handoff. No chat, no co-op sync, no always-online gate.  
**Ship test:** Full campaign/demo clear with Steam disabled; daily seed still runs from local clock / bundled rotation.  
**Builds on:** [`12_MULTIPLAYER.md`](12_MULTIPLAYER.md) · MASTER pillar Offline first

### E17 · Elegance score over speed tax
**Lenses:** Feel · Anti-wiki · Solo-first  
**Bet:** Optional stars reward Thread economy + stress peak elegance, not stopwatch shame. Slow careful seats can outrank panicked spam.  
**Ship test:** A low-Thread clear can earn top mark with longer clock; UI never puts a FOMO timer on Survey.  
**Builds on:** [`11_PROGRESSION.md`](11_PROGRESSION.md) §4 · [`07_ECONOMY.md`](07_ECONOMY.md)

### E18 · Diegetic craft nouns only
**Lenses:** Anti-wiki · Anti-purple  
**Bet:** UI speaks Brace / Feed / Oppose / Echo / Seat / Slack — never DPS, mana, rarity, chrono, nexus. Collapse copy is dry workshop voice.  
**Ship test:** String audit: zero banned chronomancy / loot ladder terms in player-facing MVP copy.  
**Builds on:** [`19_NAMES.md`](19_NAMES.md) · MASTER hard bans

### E19 · Field scarcity readable at Survey
**Lenses:** Discovery · Anti-wiki · Feel  
**Bet:** On enter, the fray announces the job type in silhouette (gap width, spill stain, beat pendulum already in frame). Players form a hypothesis before Recover.  
**Ship test:** Pause at Survey: ≥70% of playtesters correctly guess the job family (span / flow / pulse / vent) before picking Fragments.  
**Builds on:** [`06_WORLD.md`](06_WORLD.md) · [`02_CORE_LOOP.md`](02_CORE_LOOP.md) Survey beat

### E20 · Tiny carry as focus drug
**Lenses:** Feel · Solo-first · Anti-wiki  
**Bet:** 3–5 Fragment slots force planning; inventory UI is a spindle/bin, not a loot grid. Wrong picks are cheap to return.  
**Ship test:** No “full bags” anxiety VFX; players verbalize a plan before Bind more often than they hoard.  
**Builds on:** [`02_CORE_LOOP.md`](02_CORE_LOOP.md) §4 · [`07_ECONOMY.md`](07_ECONOMY.md)

### E21 · Crease → lift → seat as signature still
**Lenses:** Feel · Discovery · Anti-purple  
**Bet:** Structure stand juice is three beats of weight (crease, lift, seat + dust). That still is the capsule / trailer money shot — not a particle supernova.  
**Ship test:** Marketing kit has one mute still that alone passes “parts became a bridge/gate.”  
**Builds on:** [`09_VISUAL.md`](09_VISUAL.md) §3.3 · [`05_STRUCTURES.md`](05_STRUCTURES.md)

### E22 · No wiki-bait hidden recipes
**Lenses:** Anti-wiki · Discovery  
**Bet:** Structure recipes are introduced by jobs with visible port needs. Secret combinations that only exist on fandom pages are banned for MVP. Optional mastery = tighter budgets, not opaque alchemy.  
**Ship test:** Every vertical-slice Structure appears first as a job teach; community wiki, if any, is convenience — not a soft gate.  
**Builds on:** [`11_PROGRESSION.md`](11_PROGRESSION.md) · [`17_MVP.md`](17_MVP.md) content caps

### E23 · Solo authorship pride line
**Lenses:** Solo-first · Feel  
**Bet:** After first inhabit clear, the game leaves space for the player line *“I wove that — and now I have to live in it.”* No party invite prompt, no “share to friends” interstitial, no co-op upsell.  
**Ship test:** Post-clear beat is stamp + next job whisper only; social CTAs absent in first thirty.  
**Builds on:** MASTER fantasy elevation · [`16_FIRST_THIRTY.md`](16_FIRST_THIRTY.md) desire close

### E24 · Material bible as CI taste gate
**Lenses:** Anti-purple · Discovery  
**Bet:** Treat the material ratio (~60% substrate · ~25% craft objects · ~10% Threads · ≤5% kiln · ≤1% warn) as a review checklist for every hero frame, capsule, and trailer grade.  
**Ship test:** Art/audio block (ROADMAP G4) cannot pass if a purple-void plate ships in the kit.  
**Builds on:** [`09_VISUAL.md`](09_VISUAL.md) §2 · [`ROADMAP.md`](ROADMAP.md) G4 · [`18_RISKS.md`](18_RISKS.md) D5

### E25 · Ghost-self chalk, never rival pressure
**Lenses:** Solo-first · Anti-wiki · Feel  
**Bet:** Prior weaves replay as provisional chalk ghosts you may cross or ignore — pedagogy and pride, not PvP invade. Optional friend seed is a gift, not a ranking.  
**Ship test:** Ghosts never damage, steal, or softlock; turning ghosts off still leaves a complete toy.  
**Builds on:** [`12_MULTIPLAYER.md`](12_MULTIPLAYER.md) · [`08_LEGACY.md`](08_LEGACY.md) · [`01_CONCEPT.md`](01_CONCEPT.md) “same loom, different weave” temperature (retargeted to Yard craft)

1. **E02** Slack→taut phrase
