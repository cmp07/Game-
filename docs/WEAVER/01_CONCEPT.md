# The Weaver — Concept

**Doc:** `docs/WEAVER/01_CONCEPT.md`  
**Status:** Fantasy lock — concept elevation (CLOUD ONLY)  
**Product line:** Echo Lattice (Field Ledger labyrinth)  
**Job of this doc:** Name and elevate the player fantasy so every store still, demo minute, chamber caption, and rewrite slam answers one question: *Am I weaving?*

---

## 0. One sentence

You are **The Weaver** — a surveyor whose walk is thread. The labyrinth is a loom that prints your habits into walls; you escape by choosing a different stroke, not by beating chance.

```
Walk → trail → checkpoint → thread becomes architecture → walk differently → leave a cloth worth keeping.
```

That sentence is the fantasy. Echo Lattice is the toy that makes it undeniable.

---

## 1. Fantasy elevation

### 1.1 What “The Weaver” means

| Layer | Promise |
|---|---|
| **Role** | Not a hero, mage, or rogue — a craftsperson inside a printed field |
| **Material** | Motion is thread; walls are woven cloth; fossils are finished seams |
| **Power** | You do not conquer the maze. You **author** it — then must live inside what you wrote |
| **Mastery** | Stop walking like yourself long enough to leave a corridor kinder than your first draft |
| **Pride** | Recognition — “that pattern is mine” — is the dopamine, not loot or kill-feed |

The Weaver fantasy elevates habit → geometry from a systems slogan into a **craft identity**. The player should feel they are composing fabric, not solving a random dungeon.

### 1.2 North-star player lines

| Moment | Line the player should be able to say |
|---|---|
| First rewrite (Mirror Birth) | “I wove that wall.” |
| Act II | “It answered my looping.” |
| Boss portrait | “That face is my handwriting.” |
| Campaign clear | “I signed the cloth.” |
| Daily / friend seed | “Same loom, different weave.” |

If a still, trailer cut, or caption cannot support one of these lines, it is not serving The Weaver.

### 1.3 Emotional temperature

Quiet authorship. Dry cartographer voice. Ink, rust, paper grain — never combat juice, never horror stinger, never neon “chosen one” myth.

**Through-line (internal):** *Stop walking like yourself — then leave a weave worth keeping.*

## 3. Verb grammar (what the player *does*)

The Weaver’s craft is one verb chain, staged as ceremony:

1. **Survey** — enter a quiet field; chalk ghost of recent steps.  
2. **Spin** — walk; the trail is live thread on the page.  
3. **Commit** — checkpoint: the loom seals the buffer.  
4. **Slam** — transform folds thread into orange echo walls (origami crease → lift → slot → rust bleed).  
5. **Read** — the new corridors are legibly *yours*.  
6. **Rewrite** — cross your own fossil with a different stroke.  
7. **Stamp** — stars / habit title / (bosses) ledger silhouette — a finished panel of cloth.

Pedagogy stays in the Content Bible; this grammar is the fantasy temperature those systems must never dilute.

## 4. What elevates vs what thins

| Elevate (keep / deepen) | Thin (reject) |
|---|---|
| Path → walls as spectacle of self | Maze as RNG obstacle course |
| Ceremony scarce and sharp (Birth, Portrait, Nameplate) | Lore dumps, NPC guides, quest logs |
| Captions as craft stamps (“It matches you”) | RPG antagonist, faction myth, “Overseer” plot |
| Habit archetypes that *read* then *teach* | Class select, loadouts, ability bars |
| Museum of selves as archive of weaves | Achievement novel / moral quiz endings |
| Daily shared seed = same loom, different hands | Live-service FOMO feed |

**Hard rule:** narrative intensifies weaving; it never borrows RPG scaffolding to feel deeper.

## 5. Arc as cloth (four panels)

The campaign is one garment in four panels — not a world map.

| Panel | Act | Weaver job | Leave feeling… |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1 — First thread** | Induction | Discover the page can wear you | Curious, exposed — “it can read me” |
| **2 — Facing the pattern** | Reflection | See your route become a face / twin | Self-conscious craft — “I am drawing myself” |
| **3 — Cost of habit** | Pressure | Messy handwriting traps you | Urgency without horror — “hesitation fills cells” |
| **4 — Signature** | Mastery | Compose on purpose; sign | Quiet authorship pride — “I chose this stroke” |

Boss chambers close each panel as **portrait ceremony**, not boss fight: Who Walked → Portrait → Calcify → Nameplate.
