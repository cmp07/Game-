## 1. Category sentence (1000×)

> Offline craft puzzle: recover scarce Fragments, draw typed Threads, tension a Structure that rewrites a frayed Yard field. Physics is the judge. Recognition — “that span is mine” — is the dopamine.

```
Survey → Recover → Bind → Tension → Inhabit → Residue
```

**1000×** here is not “more systems.” It is **one verb chain made undeniable** across store, demo minutes 0–3, Yard climax, and gallery return — so strangers feel authorship in under ninety seconds and owners still feel named by their Structures weeks later.

## 2. Why unique vs the four traps

### 2.1 vs Noita (emergent alchemy / wand chaos)

| Noita affinity (honest) | Weaver differentiation |
|---|---|
| Systems interact; players invent | Systems interact **inside a job fence** — not an infinite reaction playground |
| Death / run drama sells clips | **No combat**, no HP, no wand loadout — collapse is craft failure, not corpse |
| Pixel alchemy surprises | Ports + Thread types are **readable before commit**; secret wiki alchemy is banned for MVP |
| Roguelike session shape | Deterministic seed; short retries; undo Tension; authorship pride over run prestige |

**Steal:** emergent *feeling* when a Structure answers the field in an unexpected-but-fair way.  
**Refuse:** permadeath loops, wand builds, elemental slapstick as the product identity.

**Store fence:** Never lead with “alchemy,” “roguelike,” or “every run is different chaos.” Lead with **stitches that hold or snap**.

### 2.2 vs craft sandbox (Besiege / Poly Bridge / Townscaper gravity)

| Craft affinity (honest) | Weaver differentiation |
|---|---|
| Parts become place | Same — **parts → relations → place** is pillar 1 |
| Placement beauty / physics toys | Weaver adds **win/lose Yard jobs** and typed Thread legality — not pure doodle |
| Huge part catalogs | **Tiny carry**, few families, jobs constrain chemistry ([`../22_DISCOVERY_UX.md`](../22_DISCOVERY_UX.md)) |
| Sandbox as the game | Sandbox is **dessert after literacy**, never the spine ([`../34_ADVERSARIAL.md`](../34_ADVERSARIAL.md) A7) |

**Steal:** mute-legible “I built that bridge/gate” stills; material honesty.  
**Refuse:** Besiege-scale encyclopedias, Workshop-first identity, cozy town-painter with no stakes.

**Store fence:** Never promise “infinite parts” or “build anything.” Promise **short jobs, fair snaps, Structures you inhabit**.

### 2.3 vs idle (Particul / prestige drip)

| Idle affinity (trap) | Weaver differentiation |
|---|---|
| Quiet, contemplative mood | Quiet **workshop** — hands always busy; Survey ≤3s |
| Number-go-up / automate | **No accrual**, no offline earnings, no prestige reset theater |
| Particle beauty as product | Particles (if any) serve **tension tells** — never the loop |
| Appointment / drip cadence | Session ends on a cleared job or gallery stamp — not a timer |

**Steal:** short evening sittings; calm material atmosphere.  
**Refuse:** AFK progress, sell-automate loops, “come back tomorrow for scrap.”

**Store fence:** Tags and copy exclude Idle. Adversarial kill-tests K1–K6 stay green ([`../34_ADVERSARIAL.md`](../34_ADVERSARIAL.md)).

### 2.4 vs cosmic (purple void / generative AI dungeon)

| Cosmic affinity (trap) | Weaver differentiation |
|---|---|
| “Void” as mystic emptiness | **Void = frayed physical gap** — torn span, starved basin ([`../25_VOID_ART_V2.md`](../25_VOID_ART_V2.md)) |
| Neon purple / nebula plate | Fiber, dust, timber, wire, chalk, **kiln copper** only |
| Chronomancy / time shards | Pulse/Pendulum = **craft beat**, never rewind ([`../03_FRAGMENTS.md`](../03_FRAGMENTS.md)) |
| LLM / AI worldbuilder heat | Offline deterministic; **no LLM runtime**; anti-AI store lead |

**Steal:** sense of space around a problem (the gap you must span).  
**Refuse:** circles-on-black prototype look, violet bloom, “Fragments of Time,” generative-level marketing.

**Store fence:** Capsule fails if it reads as cosmic indie gameslop after removing the logo.
