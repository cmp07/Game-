## 0. One-line thesis

The story is **the yard learning your hand** — a short craft mythos told only through job chalk, coach stamps, collapse voice, inhabit pride, loom-marks, and the gallery wall.

No quest log. No antagonist. No cutscene dump. The loom does not lecture in plot; it **answers stitches with place** until you seat something worth inhabiting.

```
Survey → Recover → Bind → Tension → Inhabit → Residue → the yard keeps the seam
```

**1000× ask:** make that mythos *undeniable* across trailer stills, first Structure, campaign close, and offline gallery return — without bolting on RPG lore or a cinematic cold open.

---

## 1. Hard rule — diegetic story, never cutscene dump

Weaver keeps one pure fantasy: **parts → relations → place**. Narrative must intensify that fantasy. It must never borrow RPG scaffolding, novel lore, or pre-rendered exposition to feel “deeper.”

| Allowed (diegetic craft mythos) | Forbidden (cutscene / RPG mash) |
|---|---|
| ≤1 coach line per loop beat, then silence ([`../20_ELEVATIONS_V2.md`](../20_ELEVATIONS_V2.md) E05) | Dialogue trees, NPC guides, companion banter |
| Job chalk + field scarcity as the “brief” | Quest markers, faction lore, world-map chapters |
| Seat / inhabit / loom-mark as **ceremony** | Boss lore dump, HP bars, named villains |
| Gallery plaques + ghost chalk replay | Character bible, codex entries, achievement novels |
| Ending as yard close + residue summary | Credit-scroll mythology, moral multi-endings |
| Collapse / legality lines as workshop voice ([`../04_THREADS.md`](../04_THREADS.md) §7) | VO lore, journal pages, comic interludes |
| Residue bias as the world’s answer whisper | Cutscenes that explain why the yard “remembers” |

**Store congruence:** short description may say there is “no story to grind” meaning *no RPG narrative grind*. This doc is the **emotional mythos already inside the toy** — not a second genre glued on.

**Cutscene ban (print on the wall):** If a beat needs a non-interactive cinematic, slideshow lore, or voiceover exposition to land, the beat failed. Fix the verb, the stamp, or the silhouette — do not add a dump.

## 2. Mythos core (what is “true” in the fiction)

Mythos here means **belief the player can feel without being told**. It is craft cosmology, not faction history.

### 2.1 Locked beliefs

| Belief | Player feels… | Systems proof |
|---|---|---|
| **Matter wants a seam** | Fragments are hungry ports, not loot gems | Recover + port glyphs ([`../03_FRAGMENTS.md`](../03_FRAGMENTS.md), [`../21_FRAGMENT_FEEL.md`](../21_FRAGMENT_FEEL.md)) |
| **Stitches judge you** | Wrong Thread snaps; honest Thread holds | Brace / Feed / Oppose / Echo legality |
| **Tension is the ceremony** | Commit is sacred; undo is re-pinning fiber | Seat / collapse / undo ([`../20_ELEVATIONS_V2.md`](../20_ELEVATIONS_V2.md) E12) |
| **Place answers cloth** | A stood Structure rewrites the field | Topology / flow / load / pulse / vent |
| **Inhabit completes the vow** | Building alone is unfinished prayer | Inhabit clears the job (E07) |
| **The yard keeps the seam** | Residue and gallery remember your hand | Loom-mark, bias, local wall ([`../08_LEGACY.md`](../08_LEGACY.md), [`../28_LEGACY_V2.md`](../28_LEGACY_V2.md)) |

**Through-line (internal, not always on screen):** *Seat what you can live in — then leave a cloth the yard will keep.*

### 2.2 What the mythos is *not*

| Drift | Why it fails |
|---|---|
| Ancient Weaver civilization / gods of the loom | Faction lore; invites codex and cutscenes |
| The Yard as a character who schemes | Antagonist mash; ruins quiet authorship |
| Chronomancy / void dreams / AI loom | Purple ban ([`../MASTER_GDD.md`](../MASTER_GDD.md) hard bans) |
| Echo Lattice “handwriting → walls” as Weaver plot | Separate product ([`../PIVOT.md`](../PIVOT.md)); craft lesson only |
| Moral quiz endings (“did you weave kindly?”) | Wrong dopamine; authorship pride is enough |

The fiction stays **material and local**: dust, fiber, timber, wire, chalk, kiln copper. Mythic temperature without mythology dump.

## 3. Emotional spine (four cloth panels)

The campaign is one garment in four panels — not a world tour and not EL’s habit-portrait arc reused as plot.

| Panel | Yard breath | Emotional job | Player should leave feeling… |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1 — First seam** | Hands | Discover that parts can become place | Curious, capable — “I seated that” |
| **2 — Living inside it** | Stitch | Learn inhabit is the vow; elegance matters | Self-conscious craft — “I have to walk my own bridge” |
| **3 — Cost of slack** | Pressure | Collapse and residue teach honesty | Urgency without horror — “the yard noticed my hand” |
| **4 — Cloth kept** | Signature | Compose on purpose; stamp the gallery | Quiet authorship pride — “that span is mine” |

Pedagogy (teach → remix → mastery jobs) stays in systems docs; this spine is what the player should *feel*.

### 3.1 Ceremony ladder (not boss lore)

Close each panel with a **craft ceremony**, never a villain reveal:

| Panel | Ceremony | Diegetic proof | Ban |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hands | **First Seat** | Tension holds; seat phrase audible | Cutscene of “becoming the Weaver” |
| Stitch | **First Inhabit** | Walk/route the Structure to clear | Victory fanfare over a foe |
| Pressure | **Honest Collapse → Re-pin** | Culprit highlight + undo courage | Shame reel / fail lore |
| Signature | **Loom-mark + Gallery pin** | Silhouette stamp on the Yard wall | Credits mythology crawl |

## 4. Delivery channels (story surfaces only)

Story may appear **only** on these surfaces. Anything else is out.

### 4.1 Channel A — Job chalk & Survey scarcity

The field itself is the brief. Fray silhouette announces gap / spill / seal / beat before Recover ([`../20_ELEVATIONS_V2.md`](../20_ELEVATIONS_V2.md) E19).

| Job | Pattern | Example temperature |
|---|---|---|
| Teach | Imperative + one consequence | “Span the tear. Seat before you cross.” |
| Remix | Habit warning, no new lore | “Fewer Threads. Same shore.” |
| Mastery | Ceremony ask | “Stand a gate you will walk.” |
| Residue-touched | Whisper that the yard answered | “The shed kept your scaffold bias.” |

Cap: one primary chalk line visible at Survey. No paragraph plaques at field enter.

### 4.2 Channel B — Coach stamps (≤1 per beat)

Diegetic chalk / press lines only ([`../22_DISCOVERY_UX.md`](../22_DISCOVERY_UX.md), [`../04_THREADS.md`](../04_THREADS.md) §7).

| Event | Approved temperature |

| Illegal port | “That edge won’t take this stitch.” |
| Overload | “Too much on one fiber.” |
| Starve | “Nothing reaches the far port.” |

| Inhabit clear | “You live in it.” |
| Elegant clear | “Tight weave.” |
| Loom-mark | “The yard kept the seam.” |

Silence after the line is authorship space, not missing content.

### 4.3 Channel C — Yard breaths (panel cards)

When a literacy panel begins, show **title + one blurb** as a Shed page turn — not a map briefing.

| Id | Title | Blurb (emotional read) | Must not become… |
|---|---|---|---|
| `hands` | Hands | Recover, bind, seat — the loom is a tool, not a destiny. | “Tutorial Kingdom” |
| `stitch` | Stitch | Inhabit what you stood. Elegance is fewer honest Threads. | Class/loadout story |
| `pressure` | Pressure | Slack and overload speak. Re-pin without shame. | Survival horror act |
| `signature` | Signature | Leave a cloth panel the gallery can read. | Prestige rank / skill-tree act |

Demo may end after **First Seat / First Inhabit** without spoiling Signature titles in store art.

### 4.4 Channel D — Gallery wall (memoir)

The Yard gallery is the mythos archive ([`../08_LEGACY.md`](../08_LEGACY.md), [`../28_LEGACY_V2.md`](../28_LEGACY_V2.md)).

| Moment | Story function |
|---|---|

| Ghost chalk replay | Re-watch the seat without combat fantasy |
| Weathering / pedigree (v2) | The shed aged with your corpus — offline |
| Wipe Legacy | Irreversible memoir erase — gravity, not gag |

**Voice lock:**

- Title: **Gallery** (or **Cloth Wall** if UI needs warmer noun)  
- Blurb: *Each clear leaves a seam. Browse what you stood.*  
- Empty: *No cloth yet. Seat a Structure.*  
- Signature pin line: *Kept.* — no cutscene.

### 4.5 Channel E — Ending (yard close)

The ending is a **loom close**, not a saga epilogue.

```
Signature clear
  → loom-mark + gallery pin (“Kept.”)
  → optional shared-seed epilogue job (same loom, different hands)
  → end screen: jobs stood + elegance / residue summary
  → soft CTA into Gallery
```

| Slot | Direction | Emotional job |
|---|---|---|
| Title | **YARD CLOSED** / **CLOTH KEPT** | Ceremony, not “You Win” |
| Tagline | *The yard kept your seam.* | Reciprocal craft — the toy answers |
| Summary | Structures stood, Thread economy, bias tags | Facts of your hand |
| Footer | Fiber / dust / kiln copper identity | Brand congruence |
| Demo variant | *You seated the first span. The yard waits.* | Promise without Signature spoilers |

**Do not add:** lore crawl, ancient Weaver prophecy, “years later,” New Game+ plot text inside 1.0.

## 5. Full-arc beat sheet (player-facing, skimable)

Use as emotional QA. If a build needs a cutscene to explain a beat, the beat failed.

1. **Shed hush** — tools at rest; job chalk readable; no destiny choir.

7. Yard breath **Stitch** — elegance and Thread literacy deepen.  
8. Remix jobs — fewer Threads, harder honesty.  
9. Yard breath **Pressure** — collapse comedy; re-pin courage.  
10. Residue whisper — next field answers your bias tag.  
11. Yard breath **Signature** — compose on purpose.  
12. **Loom-mark** — gallery pin; *Kept.*  
13. End screen — *The yard kept your seam.*  
14. Gallery — shelve and re-watch the cloth.
