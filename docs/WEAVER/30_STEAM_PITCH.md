## 3. Long description (About This Game) — DRAFT

### 3.1 Master copy

```
[b]A shed loom. A frayed gap. Your stitches decide.[/b]

You recover Fragments — Span, Anchor, Channel, and a handful of kin. You draw Threads between ports. You commit Tension. The Structure seats… or collapses with a readable why. Then you inhabit what you authored until the Yard job clears.

[b]What you do[/b]

Survey a field. Pick a tiny carry of materials. Bind Brace, Feed, Oppose, or Echo lines. Tension the graph. If it holds, the place changes — a bridge spans, a gate answers, a channel flows. If it fails, the snap names the culprit. Undo is craft, not shame.

[b]Why it is not another sandbox encyclopedia[/b]

The Weaver is a job toy, not a part catalog. Recipes stay small. Physics and typed ports are the judge. There is no player marketplace, no season pass, and no network requirement to remember your gallery.

[b]Features[/b]

[list]
[*] Fragment → Thread → Structure loop — deterministic, offline, short retries.
[*] Shed Yard hub with authored fields and Yard jobs (slice → full 1.0 job list).
[*] Readable tension language: slack, taut, snap, seat — see and hear the sentence.
[*] Local residue gallery: silhouettes and ghost replays of Structures you stood in.
[*] Material look: fiber, dust, timber, wire, chalk, kiln copper — no purple-void default.
[*] Keyboard and controller. Steam optional; the loom runs with the network off.
[/list]

[b]What The Weaver is not[/b]

Not a combat RPG. Not a coin pusher. Not an idle tycoon. Not a chatbot dungeon. Not Echo Lattice under a new name. There is no generative AI creating levels, art, or story.

[b]Sessions[/b]

Teach-fields clear in minutes. A natural stop sits around a short evening sitting. Dailies and deeper job boards extend play without a grind treadmill — when those modes ship.

[b]The line the game keeps saying to you:[/b]

[i]Physics is the judge.[/i]
```

## 5. Pricing rethink — why not $24.99

### 5.1 Strawman

Someone prices Weaver like a mid-premium indie systems game:

| Strawman | Claim |
|---|---|
| **List** | **$24.99** |
| Implied comps | Deep builders, long narrative indies, fat content mountains |
| Fantasy | “Craft + physics + gallery = $25 value” |

### 5.2 Verdict

| Decision | Lock |
|---|---|
| **$24.99 for Weaver 1.0** | **No** |
| **Planning list** | **$6.99** |
| **Band** | **$4.99–$8.99** (soft ceiling **$9.99** only with demo/content proof) |
| **Revisit $14.99+** | Only after a post-1.0 content mountain that reviews already call “deep” — still not automatic |

This matches [`13_MONETIZATION.md`](13_MONETIZATION.md), [`MASTER_GDD.md`](MASTER_GDD.md) §0, and risk **M3** in [`18_RISKS.md`](18_RISKS.md) (even $19.99 is already flagged as mismatch).

### 5.3 Why $24.99 fails the honesty test

| Pressure | Honest read |
|---|---|
| **Content hours** | MVP targets **~3–5 h** Yard jobs + light sandbox — not a $25 campaign |
| **Catalog strategy** | Studio fast-ship band is **~$0.99–$10**; Weaver is mid-band puzzle/craft, not $30 sim |
| **Refund window** | Steam’s 2h window punishes overprice harder than underprice; first thirty minutes is the real monetization system |
| **Comp shelf** | Baba-depth / short craft toys clear in single digits; $24.99 invites “thin Besiege” comparisons without Besiege’s sandbox hours |
| **Ops reality** | No live service, no multiplayer retention loop, no cosmetics treadmill to “justify” premium |
| **Wishlist math** | High list + early Coming Soon = wishlist rot and launch conversion pain |
| **Review language risk** | “Short for $25” / “feels like Early Access priced as 1.0” — avoidable |

### 5.4 Price ladder (use this, not vibes)

| Price | When it fits Weaver | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| $3.99 | Ultra-thin demo-like 1.0 | Leaves money if Act craft is real |
| **$6.99** | **Default planning** — full Yard MVP + gallery + offline loop | Needs mute-legible trailer + demo slam |
| $8.99 | Strong art/audio + clear content volume vs comps | Higher refund sensitivity if first 2h are cold |
| $9.99 | Band ceiling — trailer + depth proof only | Edge of catalog strategy |
| $14.99 | Post-1.0 expansion-sized mountain *after* praise | Scope tax; do not plan MVP here |
| $19.99 | Different product ambition | Already an explicit risk (M3) |
| **$24.99** | **Out of band for this SKU** | Mis-shelving; reject |

### 5.5 Launch discount policy

| Lever | Policy |
|---|---|
| Week-1 launch discount | **−10% to −15% max** |
| Training −50% waits | **Avoid** |
| Regional pricing | Follow Steam recommendations; preserve USD anchor |
| Demo | Free marketing surface — not a second SKU price test |

### 5.6 What would have to be true to *ever* discuss $24.99

All of the following — and even then prefer raising content before raising into the twenties:

1. Review-proven session depth well beyond 3–5 h without filler.  
2. Production identity at “premium shelf” (art, ear, ceremony) — not docs aspiration.  
3. Wishlist/demo conversion that already clears at $9.99 without refund spikes.  
4. A deliberate catalog strategy change away from the $10 ceiling.  
5. Human go/no-go after Next Fest / evergreen demo metrics — not a pitch-deck round number.

Until then, **$24.99 is a fantasy tax on a vignette loom.**
