**Doc:** `docs/WEAVER/13_MONETIZATION.md`

**Peers:** [`01_CONCEPT.md`](01_CONCEPT.md) · [`15_MARKET.md`](15_MARKET.md) · [`17_MVP.md`](17_MVP.md) (when present) · [`../GAME_PLAN.md`](../GAME_PLAN.md)

---

## 0. Verdict (read this first)

| Lock | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Model** | Paid premium Steam game — buy once, play offline |
| **Price band (MVP)** | **$4.99–$8.99** launch target (default planning: **$6.99**) |
| **Not in MVP** | F2P, battle pass, gacha, ads, energy, pay-to-skip, cosmetics shop |
| **DLC** | Fence only — no day-one DLC required to feel complete |

**One line:** Charge once for a fair loom; do not bolt a second economy onto a craft fantasy.

---

## 1. Why premium (not F2P)

| Pressure to go F2P | Honest answer |
|---|---|
| “More players” | Discovery on Steam for this shelf still runs on **wishlist + demo + trailer**, not install funnel hacks |
| “Live ops revenue” | Solo/small team cannot staff seasons without killing content craft |
| “Cosmetics fund updates” | Field Ledger / textile look rejects neon shop skins; shop UI fights the fantasy |
| Particul / idle comps at $1.99 | Different loop — idle expects drip; Weaver is authorship sessions |

Repo catalog band is **$0.99–$10**. Weaver sits mid-band with puzzle comps (Baba-depth craft, vignette session length) — not $1.99 filler, not $30 systems sim.

---

## 2. MVP revenue surface

| Lever | MVP? | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| Base game purchase | **Yes** | Sole revenue plan at launch |
| Launch discount | Soft yes | −10% to −15% week-1 max; avoid training −50% expectation |
| Demo (Next Fest / evergreen) | **Yes (marketing)** | Not monetized; converts to wishlist / purchase |
| Soundtrack DLC | Post-1.0 maybe | Only if audio identity is distinct enough to sell alone |
| Cosmetic / palette packs | **No for MVP** | Revisit only after Museum + look are sacred; never pay-to-win |
| Expansion acts / level packs | Fence | Only after 1.0 campaign feels finished without them |
| Season pass / battle pass | **Never (default)** | Explicit product reboot required |
| Microtransactions in client | **Never for MVP** | No Steam inventory economy |

### Completeness rule

If removing a paid add-on would make reviewers say “the game feels cut,” it belongs in the base game — not DLC.

---

## 3. Price rationale (honest)

| Price | When it fits | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| $3.99 | Ultra-thin vignette / short campaign | Leaves money on the table if Act craft is deep |
| **$6.99** | **Default planning number** — full campaign + daily + endless offline | Needs demo proof of rewrite slam in ≤3 minutes |
| $8.99 | Strong art/audio + content volume clear vs comps | Higher refund sensitivity if first 2h are cold |
| ≥$10 | Out of preferred catalog band for this studio strategy | Avoid for Weaver 1.0 |

Regional pricing: follow Steam recommendations; do not invent a custom regional scheme in docs.

**Refund reality:** Steam 2-hour window means the **first thirty minutes** (see `16_FIRST_THIRTY.md` when present) are the monetization system.

---

## 4. Explicit cuts (do not “just add a shop”)

| Cut | Why |
|---|---|
| Battle pass / seasons | Ops cost + FOMO fights quiet authorship |
| Loot boxes / gacha threads | Ratings, disclosure, brand poison |
| Energy / timers | Mobile economy on a desktop puzzle — player revolt |
| Pay-to-skip chambers | Breaks fair-toy pillar ([`01_CONCEPT.md`](01_CONCEPT.md) §2) |
| Premium currency | Implies a shop; we are not staffing a shop |
| Early Access as monetization trick | EA only if content mountain is honest — not to sell a prototype twice |

---

## 5. Post-launch money (optional, gated)

Ordered; each needs a human go/no-go after review score settles:

1. **Soundtrack** — if the earprint is brand-grade.  
2. **Cosmetic ledger frames / stamps** — zero gameplay power; Field Ledger legal.  
3. **Content pack** (new panel of chambers) — only if 1.0 ending already satisfies.  
4. **Bundle** with a future separate catalog game — never mash genres inside Weaver.

Echo Lattice’s post-launch DLC fence mindset ([`../RELEASE/POSTLAUNCH.md`](../RELEASE/POSTLAUNCH.md)) is a **process** reference, not a SKU transfer.

---

## 6. What success looks like (no fantasy ARR)

| Signal | Healthy | Unhealthy |
|---|---|---|
| Wishlists pre-launch | Steady from demo/clips | Bought traffic, empty demo |
| Launch conversion | Demo → purchase without shop CTA | “Coming monetization” language |
| Reviews mentioning price | “Fair for the content” | “Needs more acts / feels EA” |
| 30-day revenue | Front-loaded premium spike | Depends on week-4 pass sale |

Do not model Weaver as a live-service P&L. Model it as a **small paid ship** that funds the next separate product.

---

## 7. Lock line

**MVP monetization = one Steam price tag.** Everything else is a fence, a temptation, or a different game.
