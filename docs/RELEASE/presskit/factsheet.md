## Elevator (one line)

A labyrinth that rebuilds from your last thirty moves — you escape by rewriting your own habits, not by beating RNG.

## Short description (≤300 chars)

Walk a paper labyrinth that fossilizes your footsteps. Cross a checkpoint and the maze **rewrites** — origami walls slam shut where your habits mirrored. Four acts, daily challenge, stars for clean routes. Offline. It learned you.

## Long description (Steam “About”)

Echo Lattice is a puzzle labyrinth printed like a field ledger. You are a surveyor with a chest lantern. Every step chalks a habit trail. When you hit a checkpoint, the lattice folds that trail into rust fossil walls — the **rewrite slam** — and the only way forward is to stop walking like yourself.

**Features**

- **35 campaign chambers** across four acts: Induction → Reflection → Pressure → Mastery
- **Rewrite transforms** — mirror, rotate, thicken, and composed doubles
- **Origami rewrite slam** — crease, lift, slot, rust bleed (the trailer beat)
- **Stars (1–3★)** against fair par routes
- **Daily Challenge** — five seeded chambers each UTC day
- **Field Ledger art** — ink on paper, not glow on void
- Fully **offline** and deterministic from your inputs
