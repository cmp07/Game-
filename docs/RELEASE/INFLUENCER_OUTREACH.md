# Echo Lattice — Influencer / outreach list template

Copy this table into a private sheet for live emails and Steam keys.  
**Do not commit real keys or personal phone numbers to git.**

## Positioning for pitches

**One-liner:** A labyrinth that fossilizes your footsteps — escape by rewriting your habits.  
**Why it clips well:** the origami rewrite slam is a 1-second visual punch (crease → lift → rust).  
**Session length:** demo clearable in one sitting; full campaign is bite-sized acts.  
**Comp neighbors (taste, not clones):** tight grid puzzlers with strong verbs; diagrammatic aesthetics (ink on paper).

## Targeting notes

**Good fits:** inventiveness showcases, puzzle specialists, Godot indie roundups, “games with one perfect verb,” aesthetic-led short-form editors.  
**Weak fits:** exclusively multiplayer shooters, gacha/idle only, ASMR cooking, lore-essay channels that need narrative cutscenes.
