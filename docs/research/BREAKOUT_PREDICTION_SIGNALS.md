# Breakout Prediction Signals — Store Page, Demo & Next Fest Evidence

**Research window:** Steam Next Fest + indie storefront data, mid-2024 → mid-2026 (compiled Aug 2026)  
**Lens:** What *observable* store-page / demo evidence predicts the **next** breakouts — especially **simple** games (one verb, short session, clip-friendly).  
**Audience:** Solo/small team planning $0.99–$10 Steam ships ([`docs/GAME_PLAN.md`](../GAME_PLAN.md)).  
**Companion:** [`CATEGORY_RANKING.md`](CATEGORY_RANKING.md) ranks *which category to build*; this doc ranks *which signals to watch* before and during discovery events.

---

## Verdict (read this first)

Next Fest in 2025–2026 is an **amplifier of pre-existing momentum**, not a lottery that crowns random unknowns. The strongest public predictors of breakout-scale wishlist gains are:

1. **Pre-fest wishlist stock + recent velocity** (together) — survey Spearman **r ≈ 0.82–0.83** with fest-week wishlist earnings.
2. **A one-sentence, shareable hook** that strangers can pitch without playing — “grokkable in a capsule + short description.”
3. **Demo quality that converts players → wishlists (~20%+)** *and* holds **median playtime** (target ~60+ minutes when the loop supports it).
4. **An external spark** in the 14 days before fest (TikTok / creator cluster / festival pin) that Steam then rewards with chart gravity.
5. **Capsule readability at small size** — single subject, high contrast, genre signal in &lt;1s; S-tier vs F-tier capsules show **~4–7×** CTR gaps in industry capsule studies.

For *simple* games specifically: winners look like **one verb fantasies** (defuse / idle-on-desktop / type-or-die / push-coins / Russian-roulette stakes) with **clip-native chaos**, not systems-dense epics. Friendslop co-op dominates absolute Top charts; **solo simple hits** still break when the premise is instantly legible (Desktop Defender, Final Sentence, Iron Nest, XenoFeels, Nightwater).

Wishlists alone are a **leaky predictor of sales** (many top Next Fest games later convert ~5–26% of fest-era wishlisters within ~90 days). Prefer the **bundle**: velocity shape + demo attachment + playtime + organic shareability.

## 3. Wishlist velocity — how to read it

### 3.1 Definitions

| Metric | Formula | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Lifetime velocity | Total WLs ÷ days page live | Separates “old grind” from “excited market” |
| Recent velocity | WLs in last 14 days (or last week) | Steam algo + fest entry strength |
| Off-campaign baseline | WLs/week with **no** active marketing | True interest floor |

Source framing: [HTMAG — Wishlist Velocity](https://howtomarketagame.com/2024/06/04/what-is-wishlist-velocity-and-is-it-a-better-indicator-of-success/).

### 3.2 Pre-fest stock → fest earnings (Feb 2026)

| Pre-fest WLs | Median fest earnings | 70th pct |
|---|---|---|
| 0–999 | ~322 | ~485 |
| 1k–9.9k | ~1,006 | ~1,722 |
| 10k–99.9k | ~5,215 | ~9,633 |
| 100k+ | ~12,882 | ~27,661 |

**Practical threshold:** ~**2,000+** pre-fest wishlists opens a visibly higher variance / upside band. Entering under 1k → plan for median ~300, not a miracle.

### 3.3 Lifetime velocity bands vs fest outcomes

| Lifetime WL / day | Median fest WLs |
|---|---|
| 0–10 | ~358 |
| 11–100 | ~1,470 |
| 100+ | ~10,229 |

### 3.4 Recent (14-day) velocity medians by fest tier

| Fest outcome tier | Median WLs in 14 days *before* fest |
|---|---|
| Bronze (0–999) | ~58 |
| Silver (1k–6.9k) | ~628 |
| Gold (7k–9.9k) | ~5,089 (small n) |
| Diamond (10k+) | ~2,039 |

**Interpretation:** Diamond winners often already had **both** stock and velocity. Shadow-dropping for “fresh velocity” alone is a high-variance gutshot — only 1/10 of 95th-percentile earners in the Feb 2026 survey surprise-dropped days before fest.

### 3.5 Velocity as launch predictor (beyond Next Fest)

Constellation that suggests a game is a “bowling ball” rather than a feather ([HTMAG](https://howtomarketagame.com/2024/06/04/what-is-wishlist-velocity-and-is-it-a-better-indicator-of-success/)):

- Total WLs in **30k–100k+** band *and*
- Consistent **200–300+ WL/week** even without campaigns *and*
- Demo median playtime **60+ min** *and*
- Creators return for multiple sessions unprompted *and*
- Viral organic posts (order-of-magnitude: **100k–1M+** views → thousands of WLs)

Valve still trusts **dollars at launch** over any pre-launch number for popup featuring.

### 3.6 Conversion caveats (do not overfit wishlists)

| Source | Launch / post-launch conversion notes |
|---|---|
| GameDiscoverCo (~420 games, 5k+ WL launches, late 2024) | Median **~10.5%** first-week WL→sales proxy; top quartile ~18%, bottom ~4.8% |
| Public indie planning bands (2026) | Often **10–25%** launch week; lifetime **20–40%** for supported titles |
| Alinea tracking of June 2025 fest cohort | ~1%–26% of wishlisters → buyers by ~30–90 days; many remain unconverted |
| Trajectory shape | Linear organic growth converts better than spike-heavy “tourist” WLs |

**Under-$10** titles (this repo’s band) tend to convert **more units** but less revenue per buyer — good for review velocity / N&T chance if the loop is sticky.

## 4. Capsule patterns that predict clicks (simple games)

Capsules are the top of the funnel. Players dwell ~**0.3s**; genre must survive at **184×69 / 120×45**.

### 4.1 Size priority

Design **Small Capsule (462×174)** first — search, “More like this,” Popular Upcoming sidebars. Then scale to Header / Main / Vertical.

Sources: [Gamosy capsule study](https://gamosy.com/blog/steam-capsule-design), [Steam Page Analyzer capsule guide](https://www.steampageanalyzer.com/blog/steam-capsule-design-guide), [Immutable capsule practices](https://www.immutable.com/guides/steam-capsule-best-practices).

### 4.2 Relative conversion by visual pattern (Gamosy, 200+ capsules)

| Pattern | Relative WL conversion | Best for simple games? |
|---|---|---|
| Single character, face + eyes | 1.00 | Horror vignette, mascot roguelites |
| Single creature / mascot | 0.95 | Coin-pusher critters, cozy-adjacent |
| **Signature object + color** (no face) | **0.82** | **Slots, coins, towers, cards, desktop widgets** |
| Action pose, face partial | 0.76 | Action |
| Environment hero | 0.61 | Weak for simple loops |
| Logo-dominant / text-only | 0.34–0.48 | Avoid |
| Equal group shot (3+) | 0.41 | Avoid unless hierarchy is brutal |

S-tier vs F-tier in same genre: roughly **4–7× CTR**.

### 4.3 Five rules that matter for *simple* products

1. **Single subject** — one object or face that *is* the verb (shotgun, coin stack, desktop tower, slot machine, typing cursor).
2. **High contrast** — pass grayscale + shrink test.
3. **Genre signal without reading** — if covered-title strangers say only “dark/cute,” rewrite art.
4. **Logo &lt; ~25% of frame** — art sells; title confirms.
5. **Signature color you own in-tag** — do not clone Hades red-gold or the local Balatro purple unless you *are* that pitch.

### 4.4 Simple-game capsule archetypes that historically “read”

| Archetype | Reference energy | Capsule move |
|---|---|---|
| Chaos swarm / one more run | Vampire Survivors | Busy but centered subject; logo small |
| Object-as-fantasy | Balatro (joker), Iron Nest (turret), Desktop Defender (desktop corner) | Signature object + palette; UI chrome ok if legible |
| Stakes vignette | Buckshot / CloverPit / Final Sentence | Face or weapon as tension icon; high contrast dark |
| Mascot dopamine | RACCOIN raccoon, Cult of the Lamb | Cute face + ominous or arcade props |
| Graphic-simple | Brotato / Dome Keeper | Bold silhouette &gt; painterly detail |

**Anti-patterns that predict invisibility:** pitch-black mush, AI-slop tells, HUD screenshot as capsule, “WISHLIST NOW” art text, three logos, competitor-clone palette.

## 5. Demo evidence that predicts breakouts

### 5.1 Conversion ratios (Steam partner report)

| Ratio | Formula | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Demo conversion | (Played & wishlisted) ÷ Total players | **~20%** ≈ 70th pct (median ~16%) |
| Page / intrigue | (Played & wishlisted) ÷ Total wishlists | Sweet spot often **20–30%** among games earning ≥1k fest WLs |

Low demo conversion on **non-co-op** usually means trailer/capsule oversold or jank under-delivered. Co-op / friendslop often shows **low** play→wishlist ratios while still winning charts (friends piggyback one tastemaker).

### 5.2 Timing

| Demo launch relative to fest | Relative fest WL outcome |
|---|---|
| Months early | ~**2.5×** vs fest-week launch |
| Weeks early | ~1.4× |
| During fest / hours before | 1.0× baseline (high variance) |

**Still do the last Next Fest before launch.** Early demos accumulate reviews, streamer memory, and algorithm velocity; Cairn’s ~16-month demo runway + 200k+ downloads still topped charts.

Exceptions with Magic (do not cargo-cult): Desktop Defender (hours before; idle genre heat), YapYap (3 days; prior hit + TikTok), Bombanana (page ~25 days; concept virality).

### 5.3 Demo product shape for simple games

What repeatedly shows up in breakouts:

| Trait | Why it predicts |
|---|---|
| **One complete loop in &lt;15–30 min** to first dopamine | Matches HTMAG demo-length guidance; short-form creators can finish |
| **Clip in first 30–90 seconds** without tutorial walls | TikTok / Shorts distribution |
| **Readable stakes** (debt, death, one more drop, desktop while you work) | Shareable fantasy |
| **Separate demo store page** | Reviews + feedback; slight edge for informed teams at &gt;10k WLs |
| **Remove anything bad** before fest | Negative demo reviews poison conversion permanently |
| Progressive / carry-over when idle | Horripilant-style incremental demos; reduces “empty” feel |

### 5.4 Algo mechanics during fest (prediction implication)

- Days 1–2: broad baseline impressions (~same order for many titles).
- From day 3: winners get more impressions; Bronze/Silver fade.
- Widgets: Most Played, Most Wishlisted, On Your Wishlist, Trending Upcoming — **self-reinforcing**.
- Pre-fest WLs increase personalized “On your wishlist” impressions → stock begets stock.

Implication: if you are not climbing by end of day 2, expect smothering — plan external sparks *before* day 1, not during.

## 6. Store-page copy & trailer signals

Above-the-fold converts; long description confirms.

| Element | Predictive / converting pattern |
|---|---|
| Short description (≤300 chars) | **Genre noun in first ~10 words** + **one unique verb/hook** + feel; no lore dump |
| Cold trailer open | Show the verb in **&lt;3s**; delay story |
| Screenshot 1–3 | Outcome spectacle (payout, death, swarm clear), not menus |
| Tags | Tight cluster matching the one category (repo rule: **no mashup pages**) |
| GIFs in long desc | 3–4 loop GIFs beat walls of text |

Sources: [presskit.gg Steam description guide](https://presskit.gg/field-guides/steam-description-guide), [Immutable store page 2026](https://www.immutable.com/resources/guides/how-to-optimize-your-steam-store-page), [Steam Page Analyzer short description](https://www.steampageanalyzer.com/blog/steam-short-description-guide).

**Grokkability test (GDCo-aligned):** Can a stranger write your elevator pitch after seeing *only* capsule + short description for five seconds? Oct 2025 tops were almost all “snap commentary” games (wizard vandal crew / typing BR / voxel survival / co-op horror).

## 7. Simple-game breakout patterns (what will hit next)

Cross-fest pattern language for **simple** titles (aligned with this repo’s pure categories):

### 7.1 Shared DNA of recent simple winners

1. **One verb you can mime** — type, drop coins, idle-defend, defuse, spin slots under debt, aim turret.
2. **Spectacle without systems lecture** — chaos readable in a muted 9:16 crop.
3. **Session shape matches platforms** — 15–40 min runs *or* always-on idle.
4. **Theme &gt; genre taxonomy** — Iron Nest wishlisters overlap War Thunder despite being a slow puzzle; theme pulls.
5. **Hot lane timing** — friendslop, idle/desktop, stakes/horror vignette, “Papers Please-likes,” coin/slot dopamine toys.
6. **Price honesty** — budget co-op and simple toys convert better when priced like snacks (Alinea on Bombanana: “Go low”).

### 7.2 Mapping to this repo’s sequence

| Planned product | Predictive store/demo checklist |
|---|---|
| **Game 1 — Tension / horror vignette** | Capsule = stakes icon (face/weapon); short desc names *ritual/stakes* not “atmospheric horror”; demo = one full round &lt;20 min; clip death/betrayal in &lt;60s; target Buckshot/Final Sentence *format* without cloning |
| **Game 2 — Coin-machine** | Capsule = coins/tickets/mascot object; GIF of payout cascade as screenshot #1; demo = “one more drop” loop + light meta; differentiate hard vs RACCOIN clones |
| **Game 3 — Idle / particle tycoon** | Capsule = signature particle object or desktop framing (Desktop Defender lesson); short desc = automate / ascend verb; demo should *run while AFK* and show number-go-up in first minutes |

### 7.3 Leading indicators to watch on *other* people’s pages (scout list)

When hunting “what hits next,” prioritize titles that show **several** of:

- [ ] Short description is a **complete pitch** without opening the page  
- [ ] Capsule passes shrink test; object/face readable  
- [ ] Trailer cold-open is the loop, not a logo sting  
- [ ] Wishlist curve steepens **off** major Steam events (organic heat)  
- [ ] Demo reviews ≥90% with volume climbing pre-fest  
- [ ] Creators clip the same 5-second moment repeatedly  
- [ ] Tag page neighbors are *hot* but capsule palette/silhouette differs  
- [ ] Coming Soon age is months, not years of flat velocity  
- [ ] For simple games: feature list is short; fantasy is long

## 9. Anti-signals (predict *not* breaking out)

| Anti-signal | Why |
|---|---|
| Shadow demo + &lt;500 WLs + no social plan | Median outcome ~hundreds of WLs |
| Capsule is logo on black | Thumbnail death |
| Short desc is lore / “atmospheric adventure” | No verb → no share |
| Mashup tags/features (coin + horror + idle + cards) | Confuses algo + shopper (repo hard rule) |
| Demo conversion &lt;10% with “looks great, plays jank” reviews | Expectation mismatch |
| Wishlist stock from years of grind at &lt;120 WL/week | “Old” WLs often were never excited |
| Relying on Next Fest as *only* marketing week | Fest amplifies; rarely creates |
| Chasing friendslop charts with a solo single-player page fantasy | Different audience physics (and conversion math) |

## 10. Implications for “what hits next” (2026 forward)

1. **Expect fewer true cold breakouts inside Next Fest** as demo counts stay 3k–4k+. Scout for games that already show velocity *before* the widget wars.
2. **Simple, weird, legible concepts** keep punching above production value (Bombanana, Final Sentence, Desktop Defender, XenoFeels, Iron Nest).
3. **Friendslop owns absolute Top charts**; solo simple games win by owning a **micro-fantasy** and short-form distribution, not by matching co-op CCU.
4. **Demo attachment + playtime** are rising as the honest quality metrics while raw WLs inflate.
5. **Godot share is rising** (~9% of Feb 2026 demos) — engine is not a handicap for simple Steam toys.
6. For this studio’s $0.99–$10 path: optimize for **grokkable pure category + early demo + steady velocity**, then use the **last** Next Fest as a multiplier — not as the birth of the page.
