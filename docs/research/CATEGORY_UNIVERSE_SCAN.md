# Steam Category Universe Scan (2026 → 2027)

**Research window:** mid-2024 → Aug 2026 (compiled Aug 2026)  
**Repo lens:** Solo / tiny team · Windows Steam desktop · **pure category per product** · price band **$3–$15**  
**Companion docs:** [`CATEGORY_RANKING.md`](CATEGORY_RANKING.md) (condensed product sequence) · [`GAME_PLAN.md`](../GAME_PLAN.md)

This scan rates **top-level Steam genres** and **high-intent microtags** on five axes that matter for shipping small paid games—not for chasing AAA TAM charts.

| Axis | Meaning (1–10) |
|---|---|
| **Demand** | Evidence players actively browse/buy this lane (hit counts, browse intent, revenue medians) |
| **Saturation** | Supply pressure / clone density (**higher = more crowded / harder discovery**) |
| **Solo @ $3–15** | Realistic for one person to ship a complete, honest product in this band |
| **Simplicity→player** | How fast a stranger “gets it” from trailer + first 60 seconds |
| **Inventiveness room** | Room for a fresh hook without needing AAA production or IP |

**Method notes**

- **Hit proxy (HTMAG / VG Insights):** games reaching **1,000+ reviews** in a release year ≈ “Steam really liked it” (~$150K+ often). 2025: **20,282** releases → **608** hit 1K reviews (**~3.0%**). Source: [How To Market A Game — What happened in 2025](https://howtomarketagame.com/2026/01/27/what-the-hell-happened-in-2025/).
- **Audience proxy (Steamograph):** % of tagged games reaching **100+ reviews**. Useful for relative odds, not absolute “you will earn X.” Source: [Steamograph market dashboard](https://steamograph.com/market).
- **Revenue medians (Steam Page Analyzer):** typically conditioned on games that already reached traction (e.g. 100+ reviews)—**not** the median of all releases. Source: [Steam revenue by genre (2026)](https://www.steampageanalyzer.com/blog/steam-revenue-by-genre).
- Broad tags (`Indie`, `Action`, `Casual`) are **discovery glue**, not product strategies. Valve itself lists them as the most common tags on the platform ([Steam tag FAQ via Game Marketing Intel](https://gamemarketing.substack.com/p/what-do-the-steam-tag-changes-mean)).

## Macro market context (why 2026 looks like this)

1. **Release volume is high; “real Steam” is still rare.** ~20K Steam releases in 2025; ~3% crossed 1K reviews ([HTMAG 2025](https://howtomarketagame.com/2026/01/27/what-the-hell-happened-in-2025/)).
2. **Hit genres are sticky.** 9/10 of HTMAG’s 2025 top hit genres overlapped 2024—Narrative, Simulation, Horror, Idle, Roguelike, Management keep showing up ([same](https://howtomarketagame.com/2026/01/27/what-the-hell-happened-in-2025/)).
3. **Cozy language is the clearest 5-year trend** among games that made real money: “cozy” in descriptions of >$100K LTD titles rose from **0.4% (2022) → 3.1% (2025)** ([PC Gamer citing GameDiscoverCo](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/life-sim/the-cozy-game-boom-is-the-clearest-trend-on-steam-over-five-years-of-data/)).
4. **Valve re-tagged the store (May 2026):** added *Bullet Heaven*, *Desktop Companion*, *Organizing*, *Cleaning*, *Decorating*, etc.; renamed *Clicker → Incremental* ([Game Marketing Intel](https://gamemarketing.substack.com/p/what-do-the-steam-tag-changes-mean), [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/games/934857/valve-says-games-like-vampire-survivors-fall-under-the-bullet-heaven-genre)).
5. **Broad tags do not discover you.** Singleplayer ~98K games / Indie ~82K ([Steam FAQ via Cole](https://gamemarketing.substack.com/p/what-do-the-steam-tag-changes-mean)). Strategy: specific tags first ([Steam Page Analyzer — Best tags 2026](https://www.steampageanalyzer.com/blog/best-steam-tags-2026)).
6. **Platform money ≠ indie median.** Steam H1 2026 estimated ~$11.1B gross ([Alinea](https://alineaanalytics.substack.com/p/steam-is-having-another-record-year)); indie lifetime medians remain roughly **$5K–$15K** when counting the long tail ([SPA indie revenue 2026](https://www.steampageanalyzer.com/blog/indie-game-revenue-data)).

**HTMAG 2025 hit counts (filtered AA/indie, ≥1K reviews)** — Narrative 51 · Simulation 43 · Horror 39 · RPG 28 · Idle 27 · Roguelike 22 · Management 19 ([source](https://howtomarketagame.com/2026/01/27/what-the-hell-happened-in-2025/)).

## Top-level genres

### Action

| Axis | Score | Notes |
|---|---:|---|
| Demand | 9 | Largest Steam revenue bucket historically (~58% of >$1M exclusive-tag revenue per [GameDiscoverCo](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/which-genres-have-ruled-steam-a-new)); Steamograph: **56.2K** games, **24%** reach 100+ reviews, median rev ~**$2.0K** ([dashboard](https://steamograph.com/market)). |
| Saturation | 9 | ~166 new releases/month; giants own FPS/arena/BR sublanes. |
| Solo @ $3–15 | 6 | Viable as **short arcade / vignette Action**, not as live-service shooter. |
| Simplicity→player | 7 | Easy trailer language; hard to own a niche. |
| Inventiveness | 5 | Differentiation must come from a micro-hook (tension toy, physics arcade, bullet-heaven twist). |

**$3–15 takeaway:** Treat Action as a **secondary tag**. Product identity lives in a microtag (Horror vignette, Physics toy, Bullet Heaven).

|---|---:|---|

## 2027 under-hyped prediction (3–5 categories)

Criteria: (a) real demand signals in 2025–26 data, (b) discourse/hype lagging demand or **new Steam shelf**, (c) solo-feasible at $3–$15, (d) inventiveness still open.

### 1) Organizing / Cleaning / Decorating — **highest conviction**

Valve does not mint tags for ghosts. These May 2026 tags formalize a player habit the cozy boom already proved, while most indies still pitch generic “cozy farming.” A pure organizing or cleaning toy at **$4.99–$9.99** can ship faster than a Bullet Heaven and face a emptier discovery shelf for 12–18 months.

### 2) Desktop Companion — **high conviction**

Same tag-update cohort. Partial-screen / “plays beside work” fantasy is culturally timed (always-on PCs, Steam Deck docked, AFK comfort). Supply is thin; inventiveness room is large. Risk is OS/windowing polish, not audience.

### 3) Lean Management / single-site Job Sim — **high conviction**

Quietly one of Steam’s healthiest mid-core lanes (HTMAG hits + Steamograph 36% →100 reviews) while Twitter discourse over-weights Roguelike/Bullet Heaven. Underserved *relative to quality demand* if you keep the map tiny and the loop cruelly clear. Price top-of-band ($9.99–$14.99) if depth is real.

### 4) Horror / tension vignettes — **medium-high conviction**

Not “under-hyped” in absolute discourse, but **under-built correctly** at $3–$8. Horror’s 2025 hit rate rose while most supply remains either asset-flip jump-scare or 10-hour survival projects. Original stakes toys (not shotgun clones) remain a 2027 edge—aligned with this repo’s Game 1 recommendation.

### 5) Physics arcade toys (incl. coin-machine) — **medium conviction**

Less meme-saturated than Bullet Heaven, more trailer-native than Management, and solo-feasible. Post-RACCOIN clone risk is real in *coin pusher specifically*; broader physics toys (machines, satisfying destruction with goals, payout toys) still look under-served heading into 2027.

**Watchlist (not in top 5):** Tower Defense (evergreen, correctly priced as “later”), Idle/Incremental (demand real but supply noisy), Narrative micro-mysteries (writing-gated).

**Explicitly not under-hyped for this band:** Colony Sim, Immersive Sim, full Roguelike deckbuilders, Platformers, Party games.
