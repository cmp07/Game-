## #1 — Residue (recommended Game 1)

**Pure category:** Adaptive physics puzzle (generative-systems vignette)  
**Tags:** PHYS · SHAPE · CREATE · NO-LLM  

### Pitch
Sealed chambers that remember your actions as **residues** — local physical law edits. Escape (or stabilize) under physics you authored. Next chamber grammars bias toward habits you overused.

### Loop
Enter → act with tiny verb set → residues rewrite friction/gravity/stick/bounce patches → solve goal → ghost residue replay → habit-seeded next chamber.

### Why #1
- Best simultaneous hit on **generative reality**, **physics**, **inventiveness**, and **vignette ship economics**.  
- Offline, deterministic, clip-native.  
- Keeps particle/physics DNA from repo origin without becoming Particul idle.  
- Inherits short-session lessons from tension hits without cloning them.  
- Distinct from Jingjie-style watch-only alife (has win/lose + pedagogy).

### Risks
- Residue readability (must feel like *laws*, not random debuffs).  
- Scope creep into open sandbox before chamber craft is done.

### Slice / price
- Vertical slice: **4–7 weeks**  
- Price: **$4.99–$8.99**

### Comps (affinity, not clones)
People Playground / Besiege (physics authorship) · Buckshot (session format) · Baba (law-edit fantasy) · Particul (particle *taste only*)

### Non-goals
No idle prestige · no coin payout · no LLM dialogue · no Workshop-first platform

## #2 — Axiom (one-law physics chambers)

**Pure category:** One-law physics puzzle  
**Tags:** PHYS · MECH · NO-LLM  

### Pitch
Each chamber exposes **one** editable constant (gravity vector, restitution, time rate, friction field). The geometry can stay fixed; reality’s parameters reshape play.

### Loop
Read goal → tweak one law → play consequences → collect law shard → later compose 2–3 laws → optional lab sandbox unlock.

### Why #2
- Cleanest “simple unique mechanic” prestige.  
- Extremely trailer-friendly.  
- Slightly less “generative world” than Residue (parameters > authorship residue), but superb inventiveness.

### Risks
- Kitchen-sink constants before one law is mastered.  
- Comparison gravity toward Baba / physics-puzzle shelf.

### Slice / price
- Vertical slice: **3–6 weeks**  
- Price: **$4.99–$9.99**

### Comps
Baba Is You (fantasy kinship) · World of Goo 2 · Patrick’s Parabox (one-idea depth)

## #3 — Echo Lattice (habit maze)

**Pure category:** Adaptive labyrinth puzzle  
**Tags:** SHAPE · MECH · NO-LLM  

### Pitch
A corridor that rebuilds from your **last N moves**. You fight your habits, not RNG. Deterministic: same template + different player → different building.

### Loop
Move → checkpoint rewrite from move buffer → keys/exit → meta-unlock transforms (mirror, rotate, thicken).

### Why #3
- Purest non-LLM “shapes around you.”  
- Streamer-explainable in one sentence.  
- Slightly less physics-forward than #1–#2 (may underuse repo’s particle taste).

### Risks
- Can feel abstract/cold without strong art/audio identity.  
- Must avoid “random maze” perception — determinism messaging matters.

### Slice / price
- Vertical slice: **4–6 weeks**  
- Price: **$3.99–$7.99**

### Comps
PCG adaptive dungeon discourse · vignette pacing comps · contrast API titles like AI Roguelite

---

## #4 — Fulcrum Stairs (balance climb)

**Pure category:** Physics balance puzzle  
**Tags:** PHYS · MECH  

### Pitch
Climb a staircase that must not fall. Your body is part of the load. Collapse replays are the trailer.

### Loop
Survey unstable stair → place limited counterweights / ballast → climb as sim runs → reach door → share collapse ghost.

### Why #4
- Fastest inventive physics ship; highest “safe craft” score among inventive seeds.  
- Slightly weaker generative-reality claim (habit bias optional, not central).

### Risks
- Reads as “physics puzzle pack” if identity is soft.  
- Needs a sharp aesthetic/joke so it isn’t generic bridge-builder.

### Slice / price
- Vertical slice: **3–5 weeks**  
- Price: **$3.99–$7.99**

### Comps
World of Goo / Besiege clarity · PEAK-like fail comedy (different genre) · Buckshot session length

---

## #5 — Void Plinth (ex nihilo architecture)

**Pure category:** Creative building toy  
**Tags:** CREATE · SHAPE · NO-LLM  

### Pitch
Empty black plinth over an infinite sea. Place colorless blocks; a local rewrite grammar turns neighbors into architecture. Emptiness becomes place.

### Loop
Place/erase → automaton resolves silhouette → optional seed share → new grammar pack.

### Why #5
- Strongest “creation from nothing” toy fantasy.  
- Townscaper-adjacent market proof.  
- Weaker default Game 1 because **goals/stakes** must be added carefully or it becomes cozy screensaver without conversion; art excellence bar is brutal.

### Risks
- Beauty competition with Tiny Glade / Townscaper.  
- Soft structure → weaker $5 impulse conversion unless challenge packs ship day one.

### Slice / price
- Vertical slice: **3–5 weeks** (one grammar)  
- Price: **$4.99–$9.99**

### Comps
Townscaper · Tiny Glade · Shape of the World (presence growth kinship)
