# Full Steam Indie Trend Map — 2025 to Mid-2026

**Compiled:** August 2026  
**Scope:** Steam PC indie / small-team premium games (not TikTok-only virality)  
**Audience:** Solo / tiny-team creators prioritizing physics, creation toys, and unique mechanics  
**Hard constraint (repo policy):** One pure category per product — no genre mashups.

---

## 0. Executive map (read this first)

| Signal | Mid-2026 read |
|---|---|
| **Money** | Steam ~$17.7B in 2025; indie ~**$4.5B / ~25%** under Alinea’s stricter definition. Hits dominate. |
| **Concentration** | ~20k releases in 2025; ~**300** cleared $1M. Q1’26 medians near **$250**. |
| **What sold units** | Low-price social chaos (“friendslop”), systems sims, extraction-lite, clip-friendly tension toys. |
| **What flooded supply** | Cozy, idle/incremental, AI-disclosed asset dumps, Lethal Company clones. |
| **Discovery** | Next Fest is an **amplifier**, not a lottery. Pre-fest wishlists + 2-week velocity matter. |
| **China** | Huge Steam MAU; discovery via **Bilibili / Douyin / Heybox**; price 20–50% below West for indies. |
| **Deck** | Still a discovery / trust badge; **new hardware demand cooled** after mid-2026 price hikes (estimates, not Valve numbers). |
| **AI tags** | Disclosure common (~20–30% of new releases by 2025–mid-2026 depending on source). Correlates with weak review traction; code tools no longer require disclosure (Jan 2026 rewrite). |

**Bottom line for a solo inventor:** The platform pays for *legible novelty* at fair prices — especially toys that produce clip-able failure, readable systems, or creation loops. It does not pay for “another cozy farm,” “another idle,” or AI-slop store pages.

## 1. Macro market: size, releases, revenue concentration

### 1.1 Platform & indie share

- **Steam 2025 revenue:** ~**$17.7B**, up ~15% vs ~$15.4B in 2024 (Alinea Analytics via Notebookcheck / GAMES.GG).
- **Indie share (Alinea, stricter “indie”):** ~**25%** of Steam revenue ≈ **$4.5B**.
- **Indie share (Video Game Insights, broader):** ~**48%** of Steam *full-game* revenue in 2024 YTD — definitionally incompatible with Alinea; always cite which lens you use.
- **Standalone indie market sizing (Mordor):** ~$4.85B (2025) → ~$5.54B (2026) projected.

Premium (paid) games still dominate Steam cash: Alinea’s 2025 read puts **premium ~78%** of Steam revenue vs F2P ~22% (F2P undercounted where games use own launchers).

### 1.2 Release flood vs hit filter

## 2. What actually drove attention (beyond TikTok)

Short-form video matters, but 2025–mid-2026 winners repeatedly show **multi-channel** loops:

1. **Twitch / YouTube long-form chaos** — friendslop physics + proximity voice.
2. **Steam demos / Next Fest** — conversion engine for games that already have velocity.
3. **China UGC platforms** — Bilibili / Douyin / Heybox for CN-weighted hits (Duckov pattern).
4. **Creator-friendly hooks** — gambling dopamine (CloverPit), build porn (automation), “one weird rule” toys (Buckshot lineage / Bombanana-style demos).
5. **Early Access as live product** — Schedule I, R.E.P.O.: ship a complete-feeling loop, update monthly, raise price later.

TikTok alone is not the map. **Clipability + friend group purchase + fair price** is the map.

## 3. Tag & keyword weather: rising, saturated, underserved

### 3.1 Supply vs attention framing (academic / data project lens)

A May 2026 Steam-tag study (Jiahua, Medium; Kaggle Steam corpus through early 2025, mature-quarter ranking) classifies tags by **supply momentum vs attention momentum**:

| State | Tags called out | Solo read |
|---|---|---|
| **Rising** (supply ↑ + attention ↑) | **Crime**, **Life Sim**, **Metroidvania** | Crime/systems fantasy validated by Schedule I; Metroidvania crowded at high craft bar |
| **Saturated** (supply ↑ > attention) | **Walking Simulator**, **Cozy**, **Cats** | Cozy is demand-real but supply-brutal |
| **Underserved** (attention ↑ > supply) | **LGBTQ+**, **Base-Building**, **Cooking**, **Building**, **Grand Strategy** | Base-building / Building interesting for systems makers — but scope risk is high |

Treat as directional (methodology + cutoff), not a shopping list.

### 3.2 Keyword frequency in *successful* store copy (GameDiscoverCo via PC Gamer)

Among games grossing **>$100k** LTD, capsule/description language shifts 2022→2025:

| Keyword trend | Approx. rise | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| **“cozy”** | +675% (0.4% → 3.1% of successful titles admitting “cozy”) | Marketing word boom; not proof of easy money |
| **“solo”** | +450% | Often “solo *or* co-op” — multiplayer reassurance |
| **“factory”** | +325% | Automation / management appetite on PC |
| **“adult”** | +314% | Mature themes / sim edges |
| **“expand” / “shop”** | Rising | Progression + economy language |

### 3.3 Cozy: demand up, median outcome down

- Steam **Cozy** tag: **543** releases in **2025** alone vs **698** cumulative in all prior years (SteamDB via Destructoid / Overbaked Studio).
- Adjacent 2025 supply: ~**966** life-sim, ~**350** farming-sim dated releases (same reporting chain).
- Tag-trend work flags Cozy as **saturated**: supply growth outrunning attention.

**Verdict:** Cozy is a *player mood*, not a *solo business plan*, unless the systems hook is sharp enough to stand without the word “cozy.”

### 3.4 Idle / incremental: fivefold supply, meat-grinder economics

- App2top / WN Hub (May 2026): idle/incremental count on Steam grew roughly **5× in two years**; premium report details median revenue & retention (paywalled — treat directionally).
- Practitioner signal (Filatov, Jul 2026): mobile idle talent *not* simply migrating; AI lowered *shipping* cost → **more entries, same attention**, “brutal meat grinder.”

**Verdict:** Idle is excellent as **systems practice / catalog filler** ($2–$5), poor as “this will be my Schedule I.”

## 4. Price bands that matter in 2025–mid-2026

Psychological tiers (synthesized from GTSTU 2026 pricing analysis, StraySpark monetization notes, GameDiscoverCo median-launch commentary, and observed breakouts):

| Band (USD) | Role | Works when… | Fails when… |
|---|---|---|---|
| **$0.99–$4.99** | Impulse / experiment / idle / vignette | Sub-2h toy, particle idle, meme demo→paid | Looks like AI filler; no hook |
| **$5–$9.99** | **Friendslop / viral co-op default** | PEAK (~$8), many co-op horrors (~$10) | Thin clone of last month’s hit |
| **$10–$14.99** | Short premium / roguelite dopamine | CloverPit (~$10), RACCOIN (~$12) | Overpromises “40h RPG” |
| **$15–$19.99** | **Systems sim sweet spot** | Schedule I (~$20), Duckov (~$18) | Weak demo; no update cadence |
| **$20–$29.99** | Deep single-player / strategy | Proven craft or pedigree | New IP, low wishlists |
| **$30+** | High-bar / franchise | Rare for first-time solo | Almost never |

Additional pricing facts:

- Median launch price among top Steam launches drifted **down** (~$23.7 early 2023 → ~$20.4 late 2025 per GameDiscoverCo cited by Game Studio Unlocked) — cheaper viral co-op pulls the median.
- PEAK-team research (cited by GTSTU): **$5 / $7 / $8** feel similar; crossing **$10** changes the decision.
- Underpricing a 15–30h game at $5–$10 often **reduces** total revenue vs $15–$20 (StraySpark synthesis of VG Insights / postmortems).
- **China regional pricing:** commonly **20–37%** below Western list (Asia Game Buzz / Evolve PR); indie mass-acceptance often discussed around **≤100 RMB** unless culture-premium AAA.

**Repo-aligned band ($0.99–$10):** Still valid for vignettes, coin toys, idles, and friendslop-scale experiments — but **systems games with depth** should consider **$15–$20** if the loop supports it.

## 5. China: not optional, not “Steam China ISBN”

### 5.1 Scale

From Asia Game Buzz / GameDev Reports (2025 data, published Jun 2026):

- China gaming market ~**$51.8B**; **683M+** players.
- **Steam audience in China ~42.4M** MAU vs ~89.6M rest-of-world (same report’s framing).
- Client PC games ~**$11–12B**; Steam ~**87%** of PC/console MAU share in that framing.
- Steam China (ISBN storefront) remains a **tiny curated catalog** (hundreds of titles); most CN PC players use **global Steam** (Niko / industry explainers).

### 5.2 Go-to-market realities for indies

| Lever | Practice |
|---|---|
| Discovery | **Bilibili, Douyin, Heybox** first; Xiaohongshu / Weibo secondary |
| Keys / community | Heybox primary PC key channel; TapTap / Sonkwo matter |
| Events | ChinaJoy, Bilibili World (huge); smaller PC/indie shows often better for indies |
| Genres (directional) | Action/shooters ~33%, RPG/adventure ~24%, **simulation/management ~16%** of title mix |
| Localization | Simplified Chinese + CN-friendly trailer pacing is table stakes for CN share |
| Price | Aggressive regional discount vs West; cultural relevance can soften discount for AAA, rarely for indies |

### 5.3 Proof points

- **Escape from Duckov** (Team Soda / Bilibili): CN-made PvE extraction with duck fantasy; multi-million units in weeks; CN peak hours dominate concurrency early; priced accessibly (e.g. RMB 58 cited at launch).
- **RACCOIN** (CN co-dev / Playstack publishing): coin-pusher dopamine; **100k units in 24h** (dev announcement / Polygon); shows CN + global appetite for *arcade physics toys*.

**Implication for physics/creation solos:** CN players over-index on readable systems, management, and “number go up” arcade feelings — if you localize and price correctly.

## 6. Steam Deck: installed base vs new-unit chill

### 6.1 Installed base & catalog

- Cumulative Deck sales: IDC-linked estimates ~**3.7M** by end-2024, possibly ~**4M** by Feb 2025 (The Verge / secondary summaries).
- Compatibility catalog: SteamDB counts cited ~**25k** Verified+Playable by Jan 2026 (~7.5k Verified).
- Store-page practitioners still report Deck Verified as a **conversion / wishlist trust** signal; handheld-friendly genres can be **~10–20%** of sales for some indies (Steam Page Analyzer — directional, not Valve-official).

### 6.2 Mid-2026 hardware shock

- May 2026 Deck price hikes removed the sub-$400 entry tier; ASP estimates jumped sharply.
- Boiling Steam estimated ~**80%** weekly unit drop from chart-rank math; **XenoSpectrum / PC Gamer** correctly flag this as **estimate, not Valve data**. Confirmed: Deck fell from frequent top-5 weekly seller ranks toward ~9–16 in sampled July weeks.

**Indie takeaway:** Optimize for Deck (controls, TDP, Verified) because the **installed base still buys games**. Do not plan a 2026 launch around *new Deck owners flooding in*.

## 7. Early Access as the default hit path

Pattern of 2025 breakouts:

| Title | EA / live posture | Price at traction | Why EA helped |
|---|---|---|---|
| Schedule I | Multi-year EA plan, monthly content | ~$15–$20 | Complete loop day one; co-op longevity |
| R.E.P.O. | Classic 6–12 month EA | ~$10 | Physics toy + updates + streamer fuel |
| Many extraction / co-op horrors | EA + roadmap | <$15 | Community content requests |

EA works when:

1. The **core loop is already fun** (not a tech demo).
2. You can ship **visible updates**.
3. Price leaves room to **raise at 1.0**.
4. You can survive refunds (clear store messaging).

EA fails when the page promises a genre (colony sim, MMO) you cannot support alone.

## 8. Steam Next Fest: 2025–mid-2026 operating rules

### 8.1 Scale

| Fest | Approx. demos |
|---|---|
| Feb 2026 | ~3,300–3,500 |
| June 2026 | **4,000–4,900+** (record; sources vary 4.3k–4.9k) |

### 8.2 What the data says (Zukowski / How To Market A Game; Alinea; Ziva)

- Pre-fest wishlist count remains the strongest correlate of fest gains (Feb 2026 survey: Spearman **r ≈ 0.825**).
- June 2026: **momentum** (wishlists in prior 2 weeks) slightly edged raw base in standardized weights — but **both matter**; “launch page 2 weeks before fest” is a trap for first-timers.
- Median wishlists earned still healthy for prepared games; sub-**1,000** pre-fest titles got hurt hardest in the June “slop” discourse window.
- AI-slop panic: **no evidence** players fled the fest; Day 4 impressions peaked in Zukowski’s June survey. Algorithm appears to bury junk after early random exposure.
- Demo length heuristic from postmortems: **30–45 minutes** often beats ultra-long soulslike demos on completion/conversion.

### 8.3 What topped June 2026 (Alinea / trade)

Directional leaders by new wishlists / play:

- **BOMBANANA!** — asymmetric co-op bomb squad monkeys; premise = pitch.
- **Iron Nest: Heavy Turret Simulator** — methodical FPS puzzle/sim (~200k fest wishlists cited).
- **XenoFeels** — Papers, Please–shaped alien customs sim.
- **Mistfall Hunter** — extraction ARPG (Nightreign-adjacent).
- Simulation & co-op heavily represented in top-played lists; cozy demos earn praise but rarely dominate raw volume.

**Meta that still works:** Ship demo **months** early → market for velocity into fest → last fest before launch.

## 9. Vertical deep dives

### 9.1 Friendslop (the non-pejorative product category)

**Definition (industry consensus 2025–26):** Low-to-mid budget co-op PvE (sometimes PvPvE-adjacent) built for **friend groups**, emergent comedy, **proximity voice**, readable goals, often **janky physics**, usually **≤$20** (often ≤$10). Term coined pejoratively (~2025), reclaimed by studios (Aggro Crab / Landfall on PEAK; Panicore-era discourse around R.E.P.O.).

**Commercial proof:**

- R.E.P.O. & PEAK occupied Steam’s absolute unit-sales elite in 2025 (GameDiscoverCo / Kotaku).
- PEAK: built fast; **10M+** copies reported in trade press.
- Lineage: Lethal Company → Content Warning → R.E.P.O. / PEAK / RV There Yet? → 2026 evolutions (e.g. Big Walk elevating walker-talker craft).

**Why it is not “just TikTok”:** Purchase happens because **one friend buys four copies**. Streamers amplify; the product design creates the clips.

**Solo risk:** Netcode, anti-cheat-free hosting, and content updates. Pure solo friendslop is possible but operationally heavier than a single-player physics toy.

**Physics note:** The genre’s secret sauce is often **physics as comedy engine**, not horror fidelity.

### 9.2 Horror

Two profitable horror lanes diverged:

| Lane | Exemplars | Solo fit |
|---|---|---|
| **Social horror chaos** | Lethal Company, R.E.P.O., Backrooms extractions | High upside, MP cost |
| **Short tension vignette / ritual toy** | Buckshot Roulette lineage; CloverPit’s horror *mood* | Excellent solo fit |
| **Atmosphere walking horror** | Saturated “Walking Simulator” tag signal | Weak unless extraordinary craft |

Clone fatigue is real for “Lethal Company but X.” Wins came from **new verbs** (physics grab/throw identity in R.E.P.O., mountain climb in PEAK without horror).

### 9.3 Cozy / life sim

- Clearest *marketing* trend on Steam over five years (GameDiscoverCo keywords).
- Clearest *oversupply* risk for new entrants (543 cozy launches in 2025).
- Opportunity is not “more cozy” — it is **systems underneath warmth** (shop, expand, factory-lite) without promising Stardew scope.

### 9.4 Idle / incremental

- Explosive release growth; harsh medians.
- F2P + premium both present; retention math is the product.
- Best use for inventive solos: **visible particles / physics as the idle fantasy** (Particul-class), not feature-parity with mobile UA monsters.

### 9.5 Systems games (factory, crime econ, management, extraction-lite)

PC’s enduring appetite:

- **Factory** keyword +$325% in successful copy.
- Schedule I = crime as **management systems** + optional co-op.
- Duckov = extraction systems made approachable + strong fantasy.
- Next Fest sims (Iron Nest, XenoFeels) show **weird sim** still cuts through.
- Evergreen: BeamNG.drive concurrency still climbing into 2026 — proof that **deep physics sandboxes** have decade-long tails.

**Underserved-ish:** Base-Building / Building tags in the Medium study — but “underserved attention” ≠ “easy for a solo to ship RimWorld.” Prefer **narrow systems** with one masterpiece verb.

### 9.6 AI-tagged games

Policy timeline:

- Jan 2024: Steam AI disclosure begins.
- H1 2025: ~**8,000** titles disclosing genAI (vs ~1,000 in all of 2024) — VGC.
- Jan 2026: Valve rewrite — disclose **player-consumed** AI content; **code assistants / efficiency tools do not require disclosure**.
- 2025 share estimates: ~**20%** of releases (Tech Insider synthesis); some 2026 tallies claim ~**30%+** of new releases disclosed (GAMES.GG) — treat as trend, verify locally.

Performance:

- Game Oracle study (Burton; ~9.9k releases Jan–Oct 2025): AI-disclosed games saw ~**53% fewer reviews** after controls — but causation is muddy (AI use correlates with lower production ambition).
- Zukowski Next Fest work: slop volume rose; **quality-filtered discovery** still functioned for prepared pages.

**Practical rule for this repo:** Use AI privately for productivity if you want; **do not ship player-facing AI mush** that forces a scarlet-letter page unless the game’s value is obviously not the assets.

## 10. Engine / production side signals

- Godot share of Next Fest demos: ~**6% → ~9%** (Feb 2025→Feb 2026; Ziva / gamediscover.co scan) — still minority vs Unity (~52%) / Unreal (~18%) in that sample.
- Solo developers rising in GDC survey (18%→21%).
- Marketing often **30–50%** of total project spend in modern indie budgets (trade compilations) — discovery is the tax.

## 11. Anti-patterns (what the data says to avoid as a *first* product)

1. **Generic cozy farm / cats / walking sim** in a saturated supply wave.  
2. **Idle without a unique visible fantasy** (especially AI-asset idle).  
3. **Friendslop clone** of last quarter’s verb (no new comedy physics).  
4. **RimWorld / Cities / Palworld scope** at $10 with a solo roadmap.  
5. **Relying on Next Fest with <1k wishlists** and no demo momentum.  
6. **Store pages that look AI-generated** (capsule, trailer, screenshots).  
7. **Genre mashup store pages** (repo hard rule — and market confuses buyers).  
8. **Pricing a deep systems game at $2.99** “to get reviews” (usually destroys revenue and prestige).

## 12. Top 8 opportunity lanes for inventive solo creators  
### (physics · creation · unique mechanics — pure categories only)

Ranked for **solo shipability**, **2025–mid-2026 demand evidence**, and **fit to inventive systems taste**. Each lane is a **separate product**, not a hybrid.

---

### Lane 1 — Tension / stakes vignette (short paid horror-adjacent toy)
**Pure category:** Psychological / tension game (single session fantasy).  
**Why now:** Buckshot-class proof that a *tiny* readable rule set + clip outcomes can punch far above content volume; horror mood without friendslop netcode.  
**Price:** **$2.99–$7.99**.  
**Physics/creation angle:** One physical or procedural system that escalates (weight, timing, fragile objects, sealed-room gadgets) — not a 10-hour narrative.  
**Watch-outs:** Do not clone shotgun roulette; originality is the product.  
**Evidence link:** Short-form tension hits; Next Fest love for instantly legible premises (Bombanana pattern at larger scale).

---

### Lane 2 — Arcade physics machine (coin / ticket / payout toy)
**Pure category:** Physics simulation / arcade.  
**Why now:** RACCOIN’s 100k/day open; The Coin Game lineage; CN + global “dopamine machine” appetite. Post-RACCOIN clones will flood — **different machine fantasy** required.  
**Price:** **$5–$12**.  
**Physics/creation angle:** Authentic contact physics, juicy cascading failure, upgrade meta that does *not* become a full roguelike deckbuilder unless that *is* the pure pitch.  
**Watch-outs:** Thin reskins die; demo must show the cascade in 15 seconds.

---

### Lane 3 — Solo-first physics sandbox toy (creative chaos, not UGC platform)
**Pure category:** Sandbox / physics simulation.  
**Why now:** BeamNG’s long-tail concurrency; 2025–26 wave of physics sandboxes (Physics World: Evolution, Ludicrum, etc.); players buy *verbs*, not stories.  
**Price:** **$4.99–$14.99** (or F2P only if monetization is honest).  
**Physics/creation angle:** One exceptional simulation niche (soft bodies, fluids, vehicles, destruction, gadgets) with saveable setups / challenge rooms.  
**Watch-outs:** Competing with Garry’s Mod / BeamNG on breadth is suicide — **narrow mastery** wins.

---

### Lane 4 — Crime / shady business systems sim (single-player first)
**Pure category:** Simulation / management (crime fantasy).  
**Why now:** Crime tag rising; Schedule I proved management + taboo fantasy + fair price. Solo can ship if scope is **one city loop**, not GTA.  
**Price:** **$14.99–$19.99**.  
**Physics/creation angle:** Production chains, logistics, risk meters; optional later co-op — but **v1 must be great alone**.  
**Watch-outs:** Rating boards / regional removals; do not promise open-world action.

---

### Lane 5 — Weird job sim (Papers, Please descendants with a new workplace)
**Pure category:** Simulation (desk / duty / inspection).  
**Why now:** XenoFeels-class Next Fest traction; CN + global love for rule- folowing under pressure; small content surface, high design inventiveness.  
**Price:** **$7.99–$14.99**.  
**Physics/creation angle:** Physical evidence props, stamp machines, conveyor inspections, gadget UI as game feel.  
**Watch-outs:** Writing/localization load; keep the rulebook inventively systemic, not novel-length.

---

### Lane 6 — Particle / elemental creation idle (visible systems, not spreadsheet skin)
**Pure category:** Idle / incremental.  
**Why now:** Idle supply is brutal, *but* Particul-class “watch the matter move” still differentiates from mobile ports. Fastest code path for a catalog ship.  
**Price:** **$1.99–$4.99**.  
**Physics/creation angle:** Falling sand, fluids, reactions, automations you can *see*. Prestige that changes physics rules.  
**Watch-outs:** AI art idle clones; F2P pay-to-win expectations — prefer premium clean.

---

### Lane 7 — Narrow automation / factory puzzle (not Factorio-scale)
**Pure category:** Automation / base building (scoped).  
**Why now:** “Factory” language booming in successful copy; Base-Building/Building flagged underserved vs attention; Mid-market between cozy town and spreadsheet hell is repeatedly called empty.  
**Price:** **$12.99–$24.99** depending on depth.  
**Physics/creation angle:** Belts, liquids, heat, spatial constraints (sky-islands, grids) — one logistics fantasy done beautifully.  
**Watch-outs:** Scope creep is the genre’s graveyard. Cap the tech tree ruthlessly.

---

### Lane 8 — Extraction-lite PvE loop (solo or optional co-op) with a strong fantasy skin
**Pure category:** Action / adventure extraction (PvE).  
**Why now:** Duckov showed accessible extraction + charming fantasy can do multi-million units; clone wave of R.E.P.O. proves hunger for *loot under threat* without Tarkov cruelty.  
**Price:** **$9.99–$17.99**.  
**Physics/creation angle:** Carry weight, fragile loot physics, gadget traversal — fantasy must be **instantly screenshottable**.  
**Watch-outs:** Full multiplayer infrastructure may exceed solo capacity; ship **excellent solo PvE** first. Avoid duck clones / literal Lethal Company swaps.
