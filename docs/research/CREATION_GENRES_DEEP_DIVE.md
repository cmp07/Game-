## Executive ranking (solo · $3–$15 · inventive first)

Ranked for **this constraint set only**. Giant genre sales do not equal “make this first.”

| Rank | Pure genre | Shipability | Demand | Inventive room @ $3–$15 | Score | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | Creative sandbox toys | High | Proven megahits in-band | Excellent | **9.0** | **Best first** |
| **2** | Factory-from-scratch *(minimal / abstract)* | Medium–high if scoped hard | Extremely high | Excellent if you invent a material | **8.4** | **Strong first** |
| **3** | Inventory crafting roguelites | Medium | Strong evergreen | Excellent (“broken build” sandbox) | **7.9** | **Strong first** |
| **4** | God games lite *(toy / petri-dish)* | Medium | Hit-or-miss; WorldBox proves appetite | Good | **6.6** | Later / careful |
| **5** | Zero-to-hero crafting *(compact)* | Low–medium | Highest raw Steam probability | Medium (content mountain) | **5.8** | Not first unless tiny map |
| **6** | Colony-from-nothing | Very low | Enormous when deep | Poor at this price | **3.2** | Career later |

**Bottom line:** Within “build from nothing,” the solo $3–$15 winners are **systems toys** and **tight automation loops**, not RimWorld-depth colonies or Valheim-scale open worlds. Steam’s crafty-buildy meta-genre is real; **scope and price band** decide whether that demand helps or kills a first game.

## Market frame (what Steam actually rewards)

### Crafty-buildy is the PC meta-genre

Chris Zukowski’s “crafty-buildy-simulationy-strategy” framing is the clearest industry map for this taste cluster: systems games where content emerges from element interactions, not bespoke “go around” levels. He argues this is where small teams can ship enough *hours* without AAA art budgets.

- Source: [What are Crafty Buildy Strategy Simulation Games?](https://howtomarketagame.com/2024/12/27/what-are-crafty-buildy-strategy-simulation-games/) (How To Market A Game, Dec 2024)
- Buyer’s guide / persistence of the meta: [Crafty Buildy Strategy Simulation Buyers Guide](https://howtomarketagame.com/2025/07/02/crafty-buildy-strategy-simulation-buyers-guide/) (Jul 2025)

### Hit probability vs content mountain

Open World Survival Craft (OWSC) posts an unusually high **hit rate** among genres (~**24.5%** of tracked titles clearing 1,000 reviews in 2024 analyses) — but those hits are usually **multi-year Early Access**, **$20–$40**, and often co-op.

- [How to Market a Game: Steam in 2024](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/how-to-market-a-game-steam-in-2024) (Byshonkov summarizing Zukowski): OWSC ~24.47% success rate; farming ~20.83%
- [WN Hub summary of H1 2024 hits](https://wnhub.io/news/stores-and-publishing/item-44221): horror led absolute hit count; OWSC had 16 games ≥1k reviews in H1 2024
- [Aurelia / Zukowski “What Games Are Selling”](https://www.aureliastudio.com/post/what-games-are-selling-on-steam): “Crafty-buildy-simulationy” + horror as persistent Steam demand

**Implication for $3–$15 first game:** high OWSC *probability* does **not** mean “clone Valheim for $9.99.” Players in that tag expect depth, map volume, and often multiplayer. The inventive small play is to steal the *fantasy* (start poor → build power) and ship it as a **toy, factory, or run-based build sandbox**.

### Overall Steam harshness

Only ~**2.4%** of 2024 indie/AA releases cleared the 1,000-review “success” bar in Byshonkov’s writeup — genre choice matters, but so does wishlist, demo, and launch conversion.

## Genre 1 — Creative sandbox toys

### Fantasy (pure)

One toybox. Emergent physics / materials / destruction / construction. Goals optional or soft. The joy is **tinkering** and posting the result.

### What sells

| Comp | Price band | Signal | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| [People Playground](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1118200/People_Playground/) | **$9.99** | ~175k–296k reviews; Overwhelmingly Positive | Solo-scale physics toy; Workshop; YouTube machine |
| [Besiege](https://store.steampowered.com/app/346010/Besiege/) | **~$15** | ~50k+ reviews; Very/Overwhelmingly Positive | Machine-building sandbox; campaign optional |
| [Teardown](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1167630/Teardown/) | $30 | Multi-million units (est.); ~$44–54M+ gross (est.) | **Too big / wrong price** as a first-game template; proves destruction sandbox demand |

People Playground is the clearest **in-band** proof: inventive systems + clipability + $10, not a content RPG.

Estimated sales vary wildly by model (SteamData.AI / Raijin / Boxleiter multipliers disagree by millions). Treat review count + price + Workshop/YouTube as the reliable signal, not any single revenue scraper.

### What’s too big

- Fully destructible 3D voxel worlds with heist campaigns (Teardown-class engineering)
- “Everything simulator” with AAA fidelity
- Live-service sandbox platforms

### Inventive + small (solo $3–$15)

Ship **one** simulation thesis hard:

- One material rule set (fire/electricity/fluids/magnetism) that surprises
- One construction grammar (blocks + joints + actuators)
- Soft goals / sandbox challenges, not a 40-hour story map
- Workshop or shareable contraptions if time allows (huge retention multiplier)

**Price:** $4.99–$12.99. Demo must show emergence in &lt;2 minutes.

### Score vs constraint

**#1 for inventive first.** Matches creation + tinkering, fits price, systems-not-content, proven store pages in-band.

## Genre 2 — Factory-from-scratch

### Fantasy (pure)

Empty map → extract → process → automate → scale. The factory *is* the progression.

### What sells (giants vs inventively small)

| Comp | Price | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Factorio | ~$35 | Genre king; years of polish; **do not clone as game 1** |
| [Satisfactory](https://store.steampowered.com/app/526870/Satisfactory/) | ~$40 | ~5.3M Steam copies / ~$128M gross est. ([Raijin](https://raijin.gg/app/526870/Satisfactory/sales-revenue)); 3D open-world factory |
| [Captain of Industry](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1594320/) | $34.99 | Deep industrial + colony needs; EA years; wrong band |
| **[shapez](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1318690/shapez/)** | **launched ~$4 → list $9.99** | **Solo-origin proof** of inventively small factory |
| shapez 2 | $29.99 | Sequel scale-up (~700k+ sales claims in press; team grew) |

**shapez lesson (critical):** Tobias Springer started as a free web/itch toy, then Steam at a **cheap** price with **no enemies, no timers**, and a novel resource (shapes). GameDiscoverCo deep dive: web funnel → Steam hit, with the free web version still driving monthly sales years later ([Game Developer / GameDiscoverCo](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/business/deep-dive-how-shapez-io-went-from-web-game-to-1-million-steam-hit)). Press later cites ~1.3M for shapez 1 and series &gt;2M with shapez 2 ([inkl / PC Gamer syndication](https://www.inkl.com/news/steam-blocked-this-factorio-homage-s-early-access-release-5-times-so-its-developer-threw-caution-to-the-wind-with-a-full-release-instead-and-now-he-s-sold-2-million-copies)).

Raijin’s Steam-only estimate for shapez (~318k / ~$2.9M) is lower than developer/press lifetime figures — again: use **directional** evidence (solo → invent → cheap → huge), not one estimator.

### What’s too big

- Factorio combat + research + trains + megabase performance work
- First-person 3D factory on a planet (Satisfactory / Techtonica class)
- “Realistic” industry + pollution + colony needs (Captain of Industry class)
- Multiplayer-from-day-one expectations

### Inventive + small (solo $3–$15)

**Abstract the material.** Do not ship “Factorio but worse.” Invent:

- A weird feedstock (shapes, sounds, smells, light, paperclips, DNA, rumors…)
- A calm or puzzle cadence (shapez / Opus Magnum energy)
- Finite milestone hubs **or** infinite sandbox — pick one primary fantasy
- 2D top-down or isometric; belt/pipe clarity over AAA art

**Price:** $5.99–$14.99 (shapez’s cheap launch is the template; $29+ is sequel money).

### Score vs constraint

**#2.** Highest “progression from nothing” purity after toys; solo-proven if inventively scoped. Clone risk is extreme — **invention is mandatory**.

## Genre 3 — Inventory crafting roguelites

### Fantasy (pure)

Each run: scavenge / craft / fuse / equip → broken synergies → die → meta knowledge. Inventory and recipes *are* the buildcraft.

### What sells

| Comp | Price | Signal |
|---|---|---|
| [Path of Achra](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2128270/Path_of_Achra/) | **$9.99** | Solo-dev “broken build sandbox”; Overwhelmingly Positive; ~3k reviews; est. ~100k+ units (third-party) |
| [Noita](https://store.steampowered.com/app/881100/Noita/) | $19.99 | Spell/wand crafting + pixel sim; small team; often above band but pure “tinker the loadout” |
| [Caves of Qud](https://store.steampowered.com/app/333640/Caves_of_Qud/) | ~$30 | Deep simulation roguelike; **decade-scale** content — aspiration, not schedule |
| Broader roguelite market | varies | 2024 analyses call roguelikes/roguelites a durable profitable niche ([OP Game Marketing 2024](https://opgamemarketing.substack.com/p/the-2024-indie-and-aa-game-market)) |

Also note: inventory *crafting* as the star differs from deckbuilders. Keep the store page **pure** — crafting/fusion/equipment sandbox, not “StS + Minecraft.”

### What’s too big

- Full traditional CRL with thousands of items + hand-authored lore (Qud-class)
- Action-platformer combat polish + deep crafting (Dead Cells-scale)
- Online seasons / battle pass crafting

### Inventive + small (solo $3–$15)

- Few verbs, many item interactions (fusion, sockets, cooking, glyphs)
- Short runs (15–40 min) so content volume stays sane
- Meta unlocks that expand the toybox, not a second game
- ASCII/pixel/low-fi OK if systems sing (Path of Achra path)

**Price:** $6.99–$12.99.

### Score vs constraint

**#3.** Excellent inventiveness-per-hour and price fit. Slightly behind factory/toys because combat/balance + item volume still expands; discovery is harder than a physics GIF.

## Genre 4 — God games lite

### Fantasy (pure)

You are the weather / giants / petri dish. Shape land, bless/smite, watch societies react. Light or no direct unit micromanagement.

### What sells

| Comp | Price | Signal |
|---|---|---|
| [WorldBox](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1206560/WorldBox__God_Simulator/) | **$19.99** EA | Overwhelmingly Positive; ~50k+ reviews — **modern god-toy breakout** |
| [Reus](https://store.steampowered.com/app/222730/) | $9.99 | Classic lite god/puzzle hybrid; durable catalog |
| [Reus 2](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1875060/Reus_2/) | $24.99 | Very Positive (~2.2k reviews); est. ~77–93k units / ~$1.65M ([VORYTHIC](https://vorythic.com/games/5046/reus-2), [SteamData.AI](https://steamdata.ai/en-US/game/1875060/reus-2)) — solid, not mega |
| Classic Black & White / Godus | — | Nostalgia strong; commercial cautionary tales on ambition |

WorldBox sits **just above** the $15 ceiling — important: the hit fantasy is “toy god,” but successful pricing often lands $15–$25.

### What’s too big

- Full creature AI + belief systems + 3D villages (Black & White ambition)
- Multiplayer god wars / live ops
- “Simulate all of history” with deep civ AI on day one

### Inventive + small (solo $3–$15)

- 2D petri-dish powers with readable cause → effect
- Sandbox-first; light scenarios as DLC/unlocks later
- Spectate + intervene loop (WorldBox), **or** timed puzzle-planet (Reus) — pick one pure fantasy
- Avoid promising deep autonomous civs you cannot ship

**Price tension:** $9.99–$14.99 is honest for a lite toy; WorldBox shows players will pay more for a deep sandbox god. At $3–$7, expectation management is critical (short, delightful, not “ultimate god sim”).

### Score vs constraint

**#4.** Taste-aligned and inventive, but fewer reliable in-band hits than toys/factories; AI/simulation scope creeps fast; best as game 2–3 after a systems win.

## Genre 5 — Zero-to-hero crafting

### Fantasy (pure)

Naked start → gather → craft better tools → unlock regions / tech → become powerful. One character (or party of one), persistent world, crafting as the spine.

### What sells

| Comp | Price | Signal |
|---|---|---|
| Terraria / Valheim / Core Keeper | ~$20–$30 | OWSC giants; co-op often expected |
| [Forager](https://store.steampowered.com/app/751780/Forager/) | $19.99 | Compact “build base from nothing”; est. hundreds of thousands to low millions of units depending on model ([Raijin ~801k](https://raijin.gg/app/751780/Forager)) |
| [DYSMANTLE](https://store.steampowered.com/app/846770/DYSMANTLE/) | $19.99 | Post-apoc dismantle → permanent power; still above band |
| [The Planet Crafter](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1726490/) | ~$30 | Tiny team → 1M+ sold; terraforming crafting fantasy ([GameDiscoverCo](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/how-the-planet-crafter-hit-1-million)) — **scope still large** |
| OWSC genre stats | — | Best *probability* genre in Zukowski/Byshonkov 2024 data |

### What’s too big

- Open-world survival craft with hunger/temperature/bosses/biomes/multiplayer
- Hand-crafted Zelda-scale maps with crafting bolted on
- Creature collection + survival (Palworld-scale)

### Inventive + small (solo $3–$15)

Only viable if **aggressively compact**:

- One small island / one asteroid / one dungeon layer
- Permanent upgrades over life-sim survival stress
- Distinct gather verb (dismantle, terraform gauges, buy land tiles — Forager/DYSMANTLE/Planet Crafter ideas, **not all at once**)
- Accept that $19.99 is the market’s “real” price — at $9.99 you must look complete, not half an OWSC

### Score vs constraint

**#5.** Demand is fantastic; **solo $3–$15 first** is a trap unless the world is tiny and the hook is novel. Prefer factory or toy if the emotional goal is “build up.”

## Genre 6 — Colony-from-nothing

### Fantasy (pure)

Zero settlement → pawns/citizens → needs, production, drama → thriving (or tragic) colony. Management of many agents is the point.

### What sells

| Comp | Price | Signal |
|---|---|---|
| [RimWorld](https://store.steampowered.com/app/294100/RimWorld/) | $34.99 | Genre-defining; ~$188M gross est. in SteamData-style comps; years of depth |
| [Oxygen Not Included](https://store.steampowered.com/app/457140/Oxygen_Not_Included/) | $24.99 | Klei systems masterpiece |
| [Against the Storm](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1336490/Against_the_Storm/) | $29.99 | **1M+ Steam** (publisher); 6-person studio + Hooded Horse; roguelite city/colony hybrid ([Games Press](https://www.gamespress.com/en-US/Against-the-Storm-1-million-sales)) |
| Dwarf Fortress (Steam) | $30 | Infinite depth; multi-decade |

### What’s too big (for this brief)

Almost the entire successful tier:

- Pawn AI, moods, combat, medical, caravans, storytellers
- Fluid/temperature/pipe simulation (ONI)
- Multi-settlement roguelite campaigns (Against the Storm)

These **sell brilliantly** and match “from nothing” emotionally — and they are **wrong** as a solo $3–$15 *first* product. Price expectations alone fight you: cheap colony sims read as incomplete.

### Inventive + small?

Only as a **radically reduced pure slice** (e.g. three agents, one need, one production graph) — at which point marketing usually becomes “puzzle management” or “toy,” not colony sim. If you cannot defend the **Colony Sim** tag honestly, do not use it.

### Score vs constraint

**#6 / do not start here.** Aspirational career genre after several shipped systems games and a higher price band ($25–$35).

## Cross-cuts

### What “inventive” means commercially

Across breakouts, invention is **one sharp material or rule**, not a mashup:

| Game | Inventive core |
|---|---|
| shapez | Shapes as ore; peaceful Factorio |
| People Playground | Detailed ragdoll + materials playground |
| Path of Achra | Triggered-effect “broken build” sandbox |
| WorldBox | Petri-dish god powers + civ spectating |
| Forager | Idle-active loop + buy land tiles |
| Against the Storm | City-as-avatar + roguelite settlements *(team + $30)* |

### Too-big checklist (kill criteria for game 1)

Abort or defer if the pitch needs **two or more** of:

1. Online co-op  
2. Large procedural open world  
3. Deep autonomous NPC AI  
4. First-person 3D traversal + building  
5. “1000+ items” and a long story campaign  
6. List price pressure ≥ $25 to feel fair  

### Price-band map

| Band | What players believe they’re buying |
|---|---|
| $3–$6 | Short toy, experiment, meme physics, demo-like completeness |
| $7–$12 | Full systems toy / compact automation / roguelite buildcraft |
| $13–$15 | Top of “small game”; must feel generous |
| $20–$40 | Real OWSC / colony / factory — **not first-solo target** |

### Taste alignment (creation · progression · tinkering)

| Genre | Creation | Progression | Tinkering |
|---|---|---|---|
| Creative sandbox toys | ★★★★★ | ★★☆☆☆ | ★★★★★ |
| Factory-from-scratch (minimal) | ★★★★☆ | ★★★★★ | ★★★★☆ |
| Inventory crafting roguelites | ★★★☆☆ | ★★★★☆ | ★★★★★ |
| God games lite | ★★★★★ | ★★★☆☆ | ★★★★☆ |
| Zero-to-hero crafting | ★★★★☆ | ★★★★★ | ★★★☆☆ |
| Colony-from-nothing | ★★★★☆ | ★★★★★ | ★★★☆☆ |

For a **first** inventive $3–$15 ship, overweight **tinkering + creation** (toys, abstract factory, build-roguelite). Save full progression epics for higher budgets.

## Recommended pure-genre path (creation taste)

If this taste cluster — not the horror/coin/idle fast path — is the priority:

| Slot | Pure genre | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Game 1** | Creative sandbox toy **or** minimal factory | Fastest honest $3–$15 systems product; GIF/demo marketing; solo-proven comps |
| **Game 2** | Inventory crafting roguelite | Reuse item/systems muscles; run-based scope control |
| **Later** | God toy → compact crafting → colony | Climb price and AI/content only after reviews and savings |

**Hard rule (unchanged):** one pure category per Steam product. Do not pitch “god factory colony crafting roguelite.”

Decision fork for Game 1:

1. **Toy** if you love emergent physics/spectating and clip culture.  
2. **Minimal factory** if you love belts, ratios, and milestone dopamine — and you have a **non-ore** invention.  
3. **Crafting roguelite** if you love loadout synergies more than spatial factories.
