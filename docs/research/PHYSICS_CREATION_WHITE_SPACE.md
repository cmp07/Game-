# Physics, Creation-from-Nothing & Systems Toys — White Space (2024–2026)

**Research window:** commercial Steam products ~2020 peaks through mid-2026, with emphasis on **2024–2026** launches and long-tail comps  
**Compiled:** August 2026  
**Repo lens:** solo/small team, desktop Steam, preferably **$0.99–$10**, **pure categories** (see [`GAME_PLAN.md`](../GAME_PLAN.md))  
**Question:** Which *simple-to-play* versions of these fantasies still have white space?

**Related (sibling research on other branches):** physics-forward formats (`PHYSICS_FORWARD_GAMES.md`), creation/progression genres (`CREATION_GENRES_DEEP_DIVE.md`), category scores ([`CATEGORY_RANKING.md`](CATEGORY_RANKING.md)).

---

## Executive verdict

The **fantasies sell**. The **full-complexity products that own them are hard to displace**. White space is almost never “Besiege but better physics” or “another Tiny Glade castle meadow.” It is **the same fantasy with a radically thinner control surface**: fewer verbs, faster first dopamine, clip-friendly outcomes, optional goals, and a hook that screenshots/videos explain in under five seconds.

| Fantasy cluster | Complexity kings (occupied) | Simple-to-play gap | White-space grade |
|---|---|---|---|
| Creation-from-nothing (cozy diorama) | Tiny Glade, Townscaper | New *material/theme*, not new “no goals” | Medium — aesthetic lanes open; format proven |
| Constrained engineering physics | Besiege, Kerbal, Scrap Mechanic | Short puzzle campaigns / few-parts machines | **High** |
| Destruction sandbox | Teardown (~$30 voxel) | Small smash toys exist; “plan-then-smash vignette” thin | Medium |
| Emergent systems toy | People Playground, WorldBox | Non-gore property toys; micro petri dishes | **High** (non-gore) |
| Zen factory / automation | shapez → shapez 2 | Micro / one-screen automation below shapez 2 | Medium–High at $3–$10 |
| Pixel alchemy / world chemistry | Noita (hard roguelite) | Chill alchemy playground (not sand clone) | **High** |
| Falling sand | Powder Toy (free), Sandboxels (~$1) | Pure sand is crowded/free-dominated | **Low** |
| Climbing / Foddian physics | PEAK, Chained Together, Getting Over It | Solo or novel-constraint climbers post-PEAK | Medium (hook required) |
| Idle + particles | Particul (~$2) | Catalog lane; modest ceiling | Low–Medium |

**Best white-space bets for this repo’s band:** (1) few-parts physics puzzle campaign, (2) non-gore emergent property toy, (3) chill elemental/alchemy playground, (4) micro zen-automation. **Avoid as first ships:** open Besiege/Teardown-scale sandboxes, ragdoll-gore clones, pure falling-sand, PEAK clones without a new constraint.

---

## Method & caveats

- **Sources:** Steam store pages, developer-disclosed figures via GameDiscoverCo / Eurogamer / PC Gamer / Game Developer, and third-party estimators (Raijin, SteamPulse, Steamlikes, Steamograph). Estimators disagree; treat unit figures as **order-of-magnitude**, not accounting.
- **“Simple-to-play”** here means: first fun in minutes, small verb set, readable failure, demoable in a short clip — *not* “shallow forever.” Depth can exist behind the same verbs (shapez, Poly Bridge, PEAK).
- **White space** = demand signal exists + no dominant low-complexity owner in the price/scope band + differentiation possible without studio-scale tech.

## Cluster deep dives

### 1) Creation-from-nothing (cozy / toy builders)

**Fantasy:** Place almost nothing → world looks intentional → no stress.

| Title | Role | Price band | Commercial signal | Source notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Tiny Glade](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2198150/Tiny_Glade/) | High-fidelity diorama toy; “a lot from little effort” | ~$14.99 | **616k copies in <1 month** (dev-disclosed); later estimators ~516k–635k lifetime | [GameDiscoverCo](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/how-tiny-glade-built-its-way-to-600k); [Raijin](https://raijin.gg/app/2198150/Tiny_Glade) |
| [Townscaper](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1291340/Townscaper/) | Wave-function / click-to-town toy | ~$5.99 | Category proof for “no goals” | Steam; WIRED serene-builder overview |
| [Dorfromantik](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1455840/Dorfromantik/) | Tile landscape + light score pressure | ~$8–15 | Cozy + optional challenge | Steam |
| [ISLANDERS](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1046030/ISLANDERS/) | Minimal placement scoring | cheap | Distilled city-builder fantasy | Steam; [WIRED](https://www.wired.com/story/serene-city-building-games-islanders-townscaper-cloud-gardens/) |

**What Tiny Glade taught (2024):**

- Pillars stated by Pounce Light: **“a lot from little effort,” “no wrong answers,” “it’s alive.”**
- Viral trailers that *show building*, not lore; ~**1.37M wishlists** at launch; Overwhelmingly Positive.
- Median playtime can be ~1 hour for a toy and still convert — buyers support the *feeling*.
- Affinity: Townscaper, Dorfromantik, Unpacking, Banished/Manor Lords taste.

**Occupied:** Medieval/cottage diorama + procedural adornment at premium-cozy price.  
**White space (simple versions):**

| Opportunity | Why open | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| **Different material fantasy** (paper models, underwater reefs, neon ruins, winter ice sculpture, dirt/ant farm diorama) with same “amplify input” UX | Players bought the *feel*, not only castles | Art bar is the product; Tiny Glade set expectations |
| **Alive-systems diorama** (wildlife/ecology reacts; still no management spreadsheet) | “It’s alive” is cited as a pillar; room for stronger reactive life without RimWorld | Easy to sprawl into sim scope |
| **$5–$10 “sketchbook builder”** with fewer tools but sharper identity | Townscaper price band still works | Crowded cozy tags; need visual originality |

**Not white space:** Another gridless cozy castle meadow without a stronger hook than Tiny Glade.

### 2) Machine / structural physics building

**Fantasy:** Assemble parts → physics judges you → comedy + mastery.

| Title | Role | Signal |
|---|---|---|
| [Besiege](https://store.steampowered.com/app/346010/Besiege/) | Medieval machine campaign + sandbox | ~**1.1M** units / ~$10.7M gross (Raijin est.); ~53k reviews, ~95% |
| [Kerbal Space Program](https://store.steampowered.com/app/220200/Kerbal_Space_Program/) | Orbital realism engineering | Franchise-scale; wrong first-ship scope |
| [Scrap Mechanic](https://store.steampowered.com/app/387990/Scrap_Mechanic/) / [Trailmakers](https://store.steampowered.com/app/585420/Trailmakers/) | Vehicle/block sandboxes | Long EA / multiplayer expectations |
| [The Enjenir](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1800940/The_Enjenir_The_Engineering_Physics_Building_Simulator/) | Gridless 3D physics builder + ragdoll | Niche EA |
| [Blueprint Bob](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3215120/Blueprint_Bob/) (2025) | Build then climb your structure; jam DNA | Signals demand for **puzzle-sized** physics building |
| [Poly Bridge](https://store.steampowered.com/app/367450/Poly_Bridge/) (+ sequels) | Constrained bridge puzzles | Evergreen; ~0.4–0.7M unit est. depending on source |

**Occupied:** Deep part catalogs, Workshop meta-games, survival wrappers.  
**White space — strongest in this cluster:**

1. **Few-parts physics puzzle campaign** — 4–12 blocks, one goal, short levels, sandbox later optional. Poly Bridge proved the format; Besiege proved spectacle. The *intersection* (funny collapse + tiny toolkit + campaign) is thinner than either parent.
2. **“One machine, one island” vignette** — Besiege’s fantasy without 70+ block mastery; mission pack that teaches by constraint.
3. **2D structural comedy** — side-view beams/joints, score for elegance *or* chaos; easier tech than 3D vehicle builders.

**Avoid first:** Open Workshop sandbox competing with Besiege/Trailmakers/Scrap Mechanic.

### 3) Destruction / environment as clay

**Fantasy:** World is edible; tools make shortcuts; chaos is the highlight reel.

| Title | Role | Signal |
|---|---|---|
| [Teardown](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1167630/Teardown/) | Voxel heist + full destruction | Often estimated **~2.5–2.8M** units / tens of $M gross; ~$30; massive YouTube afterlife |
| [Brick Rigs](https://store.steampowered.com/app/552100/Brick_Rigs/) | Brick build/destroy sandbox | Strong long-tail (~$13–45M gross across estimators) |
| [Instruments of Destruction](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1428100/Instruments_of_Destruction/) | Vehicle wrecking + physics buildings | Mid-tier; editor/Workshop heavy |
| BeamNG.drive (adjacent) | Soft-body vehicle destruction | Owns “watch it crumple” for cars |

**Occupied:** High-fidelity voxel destruction + heist framing (Teardown).  
**White space:**

| Opportunity | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Smash vignette / score attack** — small maps, one tool set, three-star smash goals | Lower tech than Teardown; clip bait |
| **2D or low-poly “demolition puzzle”** — remove supports / place charges | Closer to puzzles than sandboxes; less clone competition |
| **Toy-scale destruction without multiplayer roleplay** | Avoid Brick Rigs’ MP expectations |

**Not white space:** “Teardown but cheaper voxels” — tech and expectation trap.

### 4) Systems toys (ragdoll, god, emergent properties)

**Fantasy:** Everything has rules; combine tools; emergence is the content.

| Title | Role | Signal |
|---|---|---|
| [People Playground](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1118200/People_Playground/) | Detailed ragdoll + electricity/heat/blood systems | Raijin ~**6.7–6.9M** units / ~$60M; 300k+ reviews class; $9.99 |
| Happy Room / assorted ragdoll clones | Simpler gore labs | Prove demand *and* clone swamp |
| [WorldBox](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1206560/WorldBox__God_Simulator/) | God / civilization petri dish | ~**1.2–1.9M** units; ~$14–30M gross across estimators; $19.99 EA |
| Totally Accurate Battle Simulator (adjacent) | Silly unit systems | Meme + systems hybrid |

**Occupied:** Gore ragdoll depth (PPG); broad god-sim (WorldBox).  
**White space:**

1. **Non-gore emergent property toy** — same “conductivity / density / heat / reactions” joy as PPG, themed as kitchen chemistry, wizard attic, toy laboratory, or rescue robotics. Clone market is *gore*; wholesome systems toy is thinner.
2. **Micro petri dish** — one biome, 6–10 verbs, watch life/war/ecology for 20 minutes; WorldBox without the content mountain.
3. **Contraption + creature toy** (no open survival) — spawn agents with traits; poke systems; share setups.

**Avoid first:** People Playground clones, full WorldBox-scale god sims.

### 5) Zen factory / automation (shapez lineage)

**Fantasy:** Factorio’s belt dopamine without combat, biters, or oil stress.

| Title | Role | Signal |
|---|---|---|
| [shapez](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1318690/shapez/) (2020) | Abstract 2D zen Factorio; web→Steam | Dev path: free web → Steam hit; Raijin ~**318k** units / ~$2.9M; Game Developer deep dive on $1M+ path |
| [shapez 2](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2162800/shapez_2__Factory/) (EA Aug 2024 → 1.0 2026) | 3D space factories | **~250–260k in ~3 weeks** (GDCo); later ~800k units / ~$13M (SteamPulse/VGI-class); ~97% reviews |
| Factorio / Satisfactory / DSP | Complexity ceiling | Own “real” factory; wrong for $10 first ship |
| Zachtronics (Opus Magnum, Infinifactory) | Optimization puzzles | Smaller unit volumes; hardcore audience |

**shapez thesis (still the template for white space):** strip stress, keep belts, sell approachability. shapez 2 shows the *same fantasy* can scale price/scope once the brand exists.

**White space at $3–$10:**

| Opportunity | Why |
|---|---|
| **One-screen / pocket factory** — 20–40 tiles, escalating shape/recipes, no infinite map | shapez 2 owns sprawling 3D; pocket format is different SKU |
| **Single-material automation toy** (only water, only light, only sand grains as “ore”) | Fresh metaphor; still belts/sorters |
| **Puzzle DLC-shaped base game** — limited space challenges as the core loop | Tobias noted puzzle appetite; few games lead with it at low price |

**Not white space:** “shapez 2 but smaller team” without a sharper constraint.

### 6) Pixel world chemistry (Noita-class)

**Fantasy:** Matter is real; fire/water/steam/gunpowder behave; spells are chemistry.

| Title | Role | Signal |
|---|---|---|
| [Noita](https://store.steampowered.com/app/881100/Noita/) | Every-pixel sim + brutal roguelite | Estimators often **~2–3.9M** units; difficulty is the brand |
| Falling-sand titles | Chemistry without campaign | See §7 |

**Occupied:** Hardcore wand-crafting roguelite with pixel physics.  
**White space:**

- **Alchemy playground (no permadeath)** — mix reagents, watch reactions, optional challenges; keep Noita’s “matter is honest,” drop the cruelty.
- **Spell-crafting toy with safe sandbox + daily puzzles** — wand/glyph building without full dig-down runs.
- **Environmental chemistry adventure vignette** — short story levels solved by pouring/freezing/burning (puzzle framing, not sandbox sprawl).

This is one of the cleanest “simple-to-play version of a beloved hard fantasy” gaps: the *sandbox joy* of Noita is under-served relative to the *roguelite*.

### 7) Falling sand / particle sandboxes

**Fantasy:** Paint elements; watch cascades; science toy nostalgia.

| Title | Role | Signal |
|---|---|---|
| [The Powder Toy](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1148350/The_Powder_Toy/) | Classic free OS sandbox → Steam Jun 2024 | Free; huge community; owns “serious” sand |
| [Sandboxels](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3664820/Sandboxels/) | Browser hit → Steam May 2025 ~$0.99 | Chemistry/life/food focus; hundreds of reviews, not millions of units |
| [Sand in a Box](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2225680/Sand_in_a_Box/) | Paid EA sand + mods | Crowded niche |
| [Particul](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4273120/Particul/) | Idle + falling particles | ~$1.99; estimators ~**14–19k** units / ~$26–35k (mid-2026) — modest catalog |

**Verdict:** Pure sand is **low white space**. Free giants + sub-$1 ports compress willingness to pay. Upside only if sand is a *presentation* for another loop (idle, puzzle goals, narrative), and even then Particul shows a **modest ceiling** unless discovery is exceptional.

### 8) Climbing / embodied physics

**Fantasy:** Your body (or chain/hammer) vs gravity; social chaos optional.

| Title | Role | Signal |
|---|---|---|
| [PEAK](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3527290/PEAK/) (Jun 2025) | Co-op scout climb | **1M in 6 days**, **2M in 9 days**, **4.5M within ~a month**; later holiday estimates into **tens of millions** of units across trackers — category-defining |
| [Chained Together](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2567870/Chained_Together/) (2024) | Chain co-op climb from hell | Viral at ~$5; multi-million unit class per SteamData-class estimators |
| Getting Over It / Only Up | Solo Foddian climb | Evergreen meme physics |
| [Blueprint Bob](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3215120/Blueprint_Bob/) | Build-to-climb hybrid | Adjacent, puzzle-first |

**Occupied:** Co-op climb-with-friends (PEAK), chain gimmick (Chained Together), solo rage climb (GOI).  
**White space:**

| Opportunity | Condition |
|---|---|
| **Solo tactile climber with a new verb** (magnet boots, sticky slime, expandable pole — not “PEAK alone”) | Must read in a trailer in 3 seconds |
| **Cozy / non-masocore climb** — low stakes, scenic, still physical | Underserved vs rage brand |
| **Asymmetric constraint co-op** (not a chain clone) | Post-PEAK clone risk is extreme |

**Not white space:** “PEAK but with different mountains.”

## Cross-cutting patterns (what actually ships)

From Tiny Glade, shapez/shapez 2, People Playground, PEAK, and Teardown:

1. **Amplify input** — one action produces many satisfying secondary effects (procedural ivy; belt throughput; voxel debris; particle reactions).
2. **Demo = product** — if a 30s mute clip doesn’t sell the fantasy, the Steam page won’t either.
3. **Toy vs game is a spectrum buyers accept** — Tiny Glade sold with no goals; shapez sold with soft goals; PEAK sold with clear win (summit).
4. **Price can be low ($5–$10) or cozy-premium ($15)** if the feel is unique; complexity kings sit at $20–$40.
5. **Workshop is a multiplier, not a MVP requirement** — PPG/Besiege/Teardown grow with UGC; Tiny Glade/PEAK exploded without asking players to be designers first.
6. **Difficulty is optional branding** — Noita/GOI sell cruelty; Tiny Glade/shapez sell safety. Both work; pick one.

## Implications for this repo

Aligned with [`GAME_PLAN.md`](../GAME_PLAN.md) / [`CATEGORY_RANKING.md`](CATEGORY_RANKING.md):

- **Game 1 (tension vignette)** remains a speed/sales lane — orthogonal to this doc’s fantasies.
- **If pursuing physics/creation/systems after Games 1–3**, prefer **ranked white-space SKUs 1–4** as separate pure products — not hybrids.
- **Idle/particle (Particul-like)** is validated but **not** the white-space prize of this research; treat as catalog practice.
- **Coin-machine** (separate plan) overlaps “arcade physics toy” taste but is a different fantasy (payout dopamine).

**Decision filter for any pitch in this space:**

1. Can a player feel the fantasy in **60 seconds** with ≤3 verbs?  
2. Is the parent fantasy’s *complexity* the barrier you’re removing (good) or its *identity* (bad clone)?  
3. Does a mute TikTok/YouTube Short sell it?  
4. Is tech inside Godot-4 solo scope without custom voxel engines?

## Appendix — one-line fantasy → simple SKU

| If you loved… | Don’t build… | Consider instead… |
|---|---|---|
| Besiege | Open Workshop siege sandbox | 30-level few-parts collapse campaign |
