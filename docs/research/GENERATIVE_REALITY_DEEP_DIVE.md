## 0. Executive verdict (read this first)

| Question | Honest answer |
|---|---|
| Is “generative reality” real as an industry wave? | **Yes** — world models (Genie 3, World Labs Marble, Decart Oasis, Roblox/Morpheus, etc.) are the 2025–2026 capital and research frontier after LLMs. |
| Is “generative reality” as *LLM / world-model runtime* a good **first Steam SKU** at $3–$10? | **No.** Cost, latency, consistency, Steam live-gen compliance, and conversion data make it a **tech-demo trap** for a first paid desktop game. |
| Do players hate “AI”? | **No.** They hate **gameslop** (low-intent gen content + weak loop). They love **systems that respond to them** when those systems are authored, constrained, and legible. |
| Does the prior idle-first / Particul-first impulse survive this research? | **As Game 1 under a generative-vision north star: no.** Particul-like idle is a solid **catalog** product (keep as a later SKU). Physics-as-spectacle wrapping a spreadsheet fails the inventiveness / generative-reality taste test. |
| Does the prior “tension vignette first” plan survive? | **As the fastest catalog path: still valid.** **As the generative-vision Game 1: revise.** Tension format is a *shipping format*, not the vision. Prefer a **pure generative-systems vignette** that still inherits short-session / clip / demo economics. |
| What should Game 1 be? | **One pure category: adaptive physics / habit-generative puzzle** — working title **Residue** (see §11 and shortlist #1). Offline. Deterministic. No LLM required. World literally reshapes from player authorship. |
| Hard rule preserved? | **Yes.** Residue is *not* idle + horror + coin + sandbox. Coin-machine and Particul-idle remain **separate later products**. |

**One-sentence strategy:** Ship the *feeling* of generative reality using **authored adaptive systems and physics**, not the *API surface* of generative AI — then use AI quietly (if at all) as a non-disclosed *dev tool*, never as the store fantasy.

## 4. The AI / world-model landscape (frontier vs shippable)

### 4.1 Spatial intelligence & world models — the real wave

Fei-Fei Li’s framing (*From Words to Worlds*): LLMs command language; **spatial intelligence** — understanding, generating, and interacting with physically/geometrically complex worlds — is the next frontier. **World models** are the proposed substrate ([Substack manifesto](https://drfeifei.substack.com/p/from-words-to-worlds-spatial-intelligence); [TIME excerpt](https://time.com/7339693/fei-fei-li-ai/)).

Concrete commercial / research artifacts:

| Project | What it is | Indie Game 1 relevance |
|---|---|---|
| **Google DeepMind Genie 3** | Real-time interactive world model (~720p, ~24fps, minutes of consistency; promptable world events) | Research / Ultra prototype (Project Genie). Inference economics hostile to $5 Steam SKUs ([DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/blog/genie-3-a-new-frontier-for-world-models/); Naavik cites ~**$100/hour** class estimates for Genie-3-like loads). |
| **World Labs Marble** | Persistent editable 3D worlds from text/image/video/layouts; export splats/meshes; World API (2026) | Excellent **pipeline / ideation** tool someday — not a gameplay runtime for v1 ([World Labs](https://www.worldlabs.ai/blog/marble-world-model); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/11/12/fei-fei-lis-world-labs-speeds-up-the-world-model-race-with-marble-its-first-commercial-product/)). |
| **Decart Oasis** | Playable Minecraft-like frames from a foundation model (no classic physics engine) | Proof of “game as model.” Consistency / cost / QA still demoware for original IP SKUs ([Oasis](https://oasis-model.github.io/)). |
| **Naavik world-model survey (Jun 2026)** | >$10B into category in ~18 months; Renderers vs Simulators vs Planners; gaming data as training fuel | Capital is **not** underwritten by indie Steam SKUs; consumer game-engine-killer remains distant ([Naavik](https://naavik.substack.com/p/world-models-transformative-role)). |
| **Inworld / Convai / NVIDIA ACE** | NPC brains, memory, on-device SLMs | Best as **multipliers on finished games** (Mantella pattern; PUBG Ally experiments). Not the content strategy of a first $5 game. |

**Strategic read:** The *industry* is building generative reality infrastructure. A solo Steam Game 1 should **borrow the fantasy and the systems vocabulary**, not rent the frontier inference bill.

### 4.2 What world models are good for *this* team (2026)

| Use | Verdict |
|---|---|
| Runtime pixel world model as the game | **Reject for Game 1** |
| Marble/text-to-3D for production prototyping | **Optional later**; art still needs human finish for store trust |

| Local small models for flavor text | Optional; disclosure + QA cost; not load-bearing |
| Classical generative systems (CA, WFC, directors, grammars) | **Primary bet** |

## 5. Gameslop vs inventive adaptive systems (the honesty section)

### 5.1 The Steam genAI flood — numbers

Sulka Haro’s ~53.6k-title census (through mid-2026) is the cleanest public picture:

- AI-flagged share of new releases: ~**10.9% (2024) → ~19.9% (2025) → ~30.8% (2026 YTD)**.  
- **60–90%** of monthly *release-volume growth* attributed to AI-flagged games.  
- AI-flagged titles reach modest success (≥100 reviews) at ~**55%** the rate of non-AI peers — ratio **not improving**.  
- Platform remains brutally hit-driven (top ~1% ≈ ~94% estimated revenue).  
- Valve’s Jan 2026 rewrite: disclose **player-facing** generative content; **dev-tool efficiency** (coding assistants) is not the focus.

Sources: [Haro Substack](https://fragwyz.substack.com/p/three-years-of-ai-on-steam); [GamesRadar / Yahoo summaries](https://tech.yahoo.com/gaming/articles/steam-study-over-53-000-160050011.html); [VGC on policy rewrite](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/valve-has-significantly-rewritten-steams-rules-for-how-developers-must-disclose-ai-use/).

**Interpretation:** GenAI flooded *supply*. It did not create a new high-converting Steam genre. Hits still look like games; the AI flag is incidental or a liability.

### 5.2 What players backlash against

| Pattern | Examples | Lesson |
|---|---|---|
| Undisclosed / sloppy gen **content** | The Alters prompt residue; Expedition 33 placeholder controversy / awards rescind | Trust > tooling |
| Live-gen gameplay that can’t keep promises | Vaudeville Mixed; AI Dungeon Steam churn; Life of an NPC tiny Mixed | Free language fights authored win conditions |
| Capsule-art AI tells + thin loop | Weekly Steam dump of disclosed asset-farms | Store page is a quality filter now |

### 5.3 What players love (and rarely call “AI”)

| Pattern | Exemplars | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| Adaptive pacing Director | Left 4 Dead; Alien: Isolation menace | Feels alive; offline; authored interventions |
| Memory / identity systems | Nemesis | “The world remembers *me*” without essays |
| Authored atoms + procedural composition | Spelunky, Hades room sequencing | Novelty with fairness |
| Physics emergence | Noita, People Playground, Besiege, Teardown | Player invents; system honors combinations |
| Narrow AI-as-mechanic + hand craft | 1001 Nights demo reception | AI is the *verb*, not the asset factory |

### 5.4 Commercial niche of “AI is the game”

**AI Roguelite** (pioneer live-gen RPG): long-tail enthusiast product — roughly mid–five-figures estimated units / ~$150k-class gross in third-party models, ~50–100 CCU band, Very Positive *among a small review base*. Proof of niche love, **not** of a mass $5 impulse lane.

**DREAMIO / token-economy adventures:** honest about inference cost; enthusiast ceiling.

**Status (mobile social AI sim):** real engagement — different channel, F2P/energy economics.

### 5.5 Design rule carved in stone

> **Gameslop** = generative *content* substituted for vision.  
> **Inventive generative** = generative *systems* (or tightly bound AI verbs) in service of a human vision.

For this repo’s first SKU: optimize for the second. Use AI (if used) as invisible craft acceleration. Do not market Game 1 as “an AI game.”

**Sandustry** (demo heat → EA 2026 with Hooded Horse): sand as *logistics & chemistry* — closer to generative inventiveness, heavier scope.

**Decision:** Do **not** make Game 1 a Particul clone to “use the particle interest.” Keep idle as a **later pure SKU**. Do **not** mash idle progression onto a generative sandbox (mashup trap).

## 8. Adaptive design that ships (Director Lite, not ML)

Shipping truth from Valve/Industry + academic EM literature:

1. **Observe** cheap signals (deaths, idle, damage, resource, path loops).  
2. **Estimate** a scalar (intensity / menace / habit vector).  
3. **Act** from a **closed set** of designer-validated interventions.  
4. **Cooldown** so the system does not thrash.

Booth’s L4D punchline still applies: adjust **pacing**, not silent difficulty cheats that steal earned wins.

**For Game 1:** implement a **Director Lite** / habit buffer — not personalized ML level gen, not LLM drama managers.

## 9. Demoware traps (kill list)

Reject any generative pitch that fails these:

1. Infinity without verbs.  
2. Trailer promises a universe; slice is fog + walk.  
3. API latency / token meter as core gameplay (wrong business for $5 offline Steam).  
4. Model-as-designer (quests that break your ruleset).  
5. Procgen sameness (NMS launch lesson).  
6. Sandbox with no share/challenge loop.  
7. “AI art” store stigma without craft proof.  
8. Scope cosplay (Spore / Dreams / Outer Wilds dressed as a weekend exe).  
9. Mashup of coin + idle + horror + creation “so it appeals to everyone.”  
10. Vertical slice cannot show the shape-around-you moment in **60–90 seconds offline**.

## 10. Inventive synthesis — what “unique Game 1” means here

### 10.1 Vision distillation (do not dilute)

1. **Ex nihilo / authorship** — making / writing the world is the point.  
2. **Physics as language** — bodies and fields answer, not menus.  
3. **One weird verb** — simple rules → surprising sentences.  
4. **Outside the box** — not a normal genre slot filler.  
5. **Generative reality** — reconfiguration around **presence, habits, and authorship**, not merely a RNG seed.

### 10.2 Two stacks (keep separate)

| Stack | Generates | Trust | Role in Game 1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Simulation generative** | Physics, automata, grammars, habit transforms | Offline, deterministic-ish, clip-friendly | **Core** |
| **Model generative** | LLM / image / world-model | Cost, variance, policy | **Out of v1 runtime** |

### 10.3 Recommended unique product — **Residue** (working title)

**Pure category:** Adaptive physics puzzle (generative-systems vignette)  
**One-line pitch:** Every sealed chamber remembers what you did — and rewrites its physics from that residue until you escape or collapse.

#### Loop (30–90s cycles inside 8–20 min chambers)

1. Enter a sealed chamber with a clear goal (reach door / lift core / hold a bridge for N seconds).  
2. Act with a tiny verb set (move, place one ballast type, ignite/cut one strut, pour a limited sand packet — **pick a final subset in GDD; do not ship all**).  
3. Actions leave **residues** — local law edits (friction patches, gravity tilts, restitution bands, sticky films) that persist for the chamber.  
4. The chamber becomes a function of your habits; solve under self-authored physics.  
5. On clear/fail, a ghost replay shows the residue map (clip engine).  
6. Next chamber’s **grammar seed** biases toward residues you overused (habit-conditioned generation — deterministic).

#### Why this is generative reality (honestly)

- The world **literally reshapes** around authorship.  
- Novelty is systemic, not LLM essay novelty.  
- Streamers can show: same chamber template, different player → different physical laws mid-run.  
- No rental AI. Aligns with the public bio (“tired of renting AI”) better than an Inworld invoice.

#### Why this is *not* a mashup

| Not included | Why |
|---|---|
| Idle / prestige / automation | Separate Particul-class SKU later |
| Coin-pusher payout fantasy | Separate Game 2 |
| Horror shotgun ritual | Tension *format* lessons only (session length, stakes readability) |
| Open sandbox workshop | Optional post-1.0; v1 is authored chambers |
| LLM confession NPCs | Different product; disclosure + cost |

#### Why this beats idle-first *under the generative north star*

Idle wins on code speed and loses on inventiveness / “build themselves” fantasy depth. Residue keeps particle/physics DNA from the repo’s origin story but makes simulation **decision-critical**.

#### Why this revises tension-vignette-first

Keep Buckshot’s *shipping lessons* (short session, demoability, $3–$8 band, clip outcomes). Replace the *content fantasy* with generative authorship — still a vignette-sized product, not a 40-hour sim.

#### Feasibility (Godot 4, solo/small)

| Slice | Target |
|---|---|
| Vertical slice | 4–7 weeks: 1 residue type + 1 goal type + 8 chambers + replay |
| MVP | 12–16 weeks: 3 residue families, 30–40 chambers, daily seed, demo build |
| Price band | **$4.99–$8.99** |
| Engine | Godot 4 desktop; 2D rigidbody + field overlays (avoid Teardown voxels / Noita full CA) |
| Offline | Required for v1 |
| Steam tags (indicative) | Puzzle, Physics, Indie, Singleplayer, Atmospheric, Experimental, Replay Value |

#### Kill criteria (stop or rewrite)

- Players can’t explain the verb after a muted 15s clip.  
- Residues feel like random debuffs rather than authored laws.  
- You expand to 12 residue types before 8 chambers feel great.  
- Store page reads as “AI generates the world.”  
- Scope creeps into People Playground workshop platform before chamber craft is done.
